Detailed User Guide

Step 1: Upload HEIC Files

Drag and drop .HEIC / .HEIF files. Multiple files are supported.

Step 2: Choose Output Format

Select JPG (universal, smaller files) or PNG (lossless, larger).

Step 3: Convert

Click "Convert All". Each HEIC is decoded in your browser using heic2any.

Step 4: Download

Click "Download ZIP" to get all converted files in one archive.

What is HEIC?

  • HEIC is the default photo format on iPhones since iOS 11
  • Many Windows/Android devices cannot open HEIC files natively
