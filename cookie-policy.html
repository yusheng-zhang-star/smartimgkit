Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, improve functionality, and deliver relevant content.

2. How We Use Cookies

SmartImgKit uses cookies for the following purposes:

Category Purpose Duration Can Disable?
Essential Theme preference (dark/light mode), language setting 1 year No (required for site)
Analytics Page views, visitor counts, traffic sources (Google Analytics) 2 years Yes
  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function properly (e.g., theme preference storage). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it. These are optional.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that place cookies on your device, including:

  • Google Analytics — for website traffic analysis

These third parties have their own cookie policies and privacy practices.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
