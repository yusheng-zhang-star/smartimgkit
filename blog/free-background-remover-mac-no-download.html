Tips for Better Cutouts on Mac

  • Use a solid background when shooting: If you shoot product photos on a plain white sheet, the cutout will be perfect. If you shoot on a busy background, the edges won't be as clean.
  • Retouch small errors: After the automatic cutout, zoom in and check the edges. If there are small spots left from the old background, you can erase them with any free image editor on your Mac (like Preview).
  • Save as PNG: Transparent backgrounds only work in PNG format. Don't save as JPG — it will fill the transparent area with white.
  • Batch process: If you have 20 product photos to cut out, upload them all at once. Our tool processes them one by one locally on your Mac, no upload required.

Common Mistakes

  • Using online tools that upload your photos: Most free background remover tools upload your photos to their server. Our tool runs locally, so your product photos never leave your Mac — important if you have unreleased products.
  • Forgetting to check edges: Automatic cutouts are usually perfect, but always zoom in and check hair, wire, or small details. It's easy to miss a tiny bit of background.
  • Saving as JPG: JPG doesn't support transparency, so your transparent PNG will turn into a white background. Always save as PNG.
  • Using the cutout for the Amazon main image: Amazon requires a pure white background, not transparent. After cutting out the product, put it on a solid white background before uploading.
