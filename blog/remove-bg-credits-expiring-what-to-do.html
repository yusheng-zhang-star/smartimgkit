What Happens to Your Credits?

This is the question everyone is asking. Here's the straightforward answer:

Will my credits transfer to Canva? ▼
No. remove.bg credits are separate from Canva credits and will not transfer. They are two different billing systems.
Will my credits transfer to Leonardo.Ai? ▼
No. The API migration to Leonardo.Ai is a separate transition. Existing remove.bg credits do not carry over. You'll need to purchase new credits from Leonardo.Ai if you use the API.
Can I get a refund for unused credits? ▼
Unlikely. remove.bg's terms of service state that credits are non-refundable. The company has not announced any refund program for the shutdown. Your best option is to use them before the deadline.
What if I have a subscription? ▼
Subscriptions should be cancelled before the next billing cycle to avoid being charged for a service that's shutting down. Check your account settings or contact remove.bg support to confirm cancellation.
What about my stored images and history? ▼
Download any images you need from your remove.bg account before December 1. After shutdown, your account and all stored data will be deactivated. There may be a grace period for data export, but don't count on it — download now.
