FAQ: Compress Images to 500KB

Can I compress PNG files to 500KB?

Yes, but PNG files use lossless compression so they won't shrink as much as JPEG. For best results, convert PNG to JPEG first, then use the target size feature.

Will compressing to 500KB hurt my SEO?

No. Smaller images improve page load speed, which is a Google ranking factor. 500KB images load 3x faster than 2MB images, boosting your Core Web Vitals score.

Why is my output still over 500KB?

If your source image is very high resolution (e.g. 6000x4000px), even minimum JPEG quality may stay over 500KB. Resize the image dimensions first, then compress to hit the target.
