The 5 hidden limits of iLoveIMG free tier

1. Daily upload caps: After around 50 total images processed per day, the free tier starts slowing down and eventually blocks further uploads until the next day. E-commerce sellers launching 50 new product photos in one batch hit this wall immediately.

2. 20MB per file limit: Any image larger than 20MB — common for modern phone cameras and professional product shots — gets rejected on the free plan, forcing you to upgrade to iLoveIMG Pro at $6/month.

3. Silent quality drops: iLoveIMG's free compression often reduces resolution by 30-50% without a visible warning. Many Etsy sellers only notice the blurry photos after their listings get rejected by marketplace reviewers.

4. All images are uploaded to their cloud: Every photo you edit on iLoveIMG is sent to their servers, processed remotely, and stored for 2 hours before deletion. Unreleased product shots, private photos and sensitive ID documents are exposed during this window.

5. Batch limits: Free users can only process 15 images per batch. If you have 100 photos to compress, you have to manually split them into 7 separate batches — slow and tedious.
