Step-by-Step Guide

Remove Image Backgrounds Offline & Securely in 3 Steps

No upload. No sign-up. No app. Open a browser, drop your photo, download the cutout. Here's exactly how.

1

Open the no-upload background remover

Go to smartimgkit.com/tools/background-remover in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).

The first time, the AI model (~30–150MB depending on quality) downloads and caches itself. This takes 30–60 seconds. After that, it loads instantly — even offline.

💡 Choose ⚡ Fast (~30MB) for slow connections, ⭐ Balanced (~60MB) for everyday use, 🏆 Best (~150MB) for fine hair detail.
2

Drop your photo into the dropzone

Drag and drop a JPG, PNG, or WebP file (up to 10MB). You can also click to browse, or add multiple photos for batch processing.

Your photo never uploads. The ISNet AI runs locally in your browser. Open DevTools → Network tab if you want to verify — you'll see zero image upload requests.

3

Pick your output, click Remove, download

Before processing, choose:

• Format: PNG (transparent), JPG (white background), or WebP (smaller, transparent)
• Type: Cutout (the foreground subject) or Mask (black-and-white alpha for post-processing)

Click 🪄 Remove Backgrounds. On a modern device the cutout appears in 2–8 seconds. Download the result, or pack everything into a ZIP for batch exports.

When to use which format

• PNG — stickers, design overlays, product cutouts on transparent layers
• JPG — eBay/Amazon listings, email attachments, anywhere transparency isn't needed
• WebP — websites and blogs (25–35% smaller than PNG at the same quality)
• Mask — if you want to composite the subject onto a custom background yourself
