DIY Passport Photos at Home: Size Guide (US, Schengen, China)

Published: 2026-09-24

A photo studio charges 30-80 RMB for a set of passport photos. You can make them at home for free in 2 minutes with just a smartphone camera and a plain wall.

Common Photo Sizes

  • US visa / passport: 2×2 inches (51×51mm)
  • Schengen visa: 35×45mm
  • Japan: 45×45mm
  • China 1-inch: 25×35mm
  • China 2-inch: 35×49mm

Tips for a Good DIY Photo

  • Stand against a white or light blue wall
  • Face the camera directly, no shadows on your face
  • Good lighting — daylight from a window is best
  • No hats, sunglasses, or headphones
