How to Make Code Screenshots Look Professional (2026)

Published: 2026-09-17

If you've ever pasted a raw screenshot of your terminal into a blog post or tweet, you know how ugly it looks — black rectangle, no padding, no shadow, no personality. Professional developers use tools like Carbon or Ray.so to make code screenshots look polished. But those tools require you to re-type or paste your code, and they process your screenshots on their servers.

The Problem with Carbon and Ray.so

Carbon and Ray.so are great, but they have three limitations:

  • You must re-type your code. You can't just drag a screenshot in — you have to copy-paste the code text, which is error-prone for long snippets.
  • They upload your code. If you're sharing proprietary code or API keys, pasting it into a web tool is a privacy risk.
  • They're heavy. Carbon takes 3-5 seconds to load on a slow connection.

A Better Way: Beautify Your Screenshot Directly

  • Rounded corners (0-30px adjustable)
  • Drop shadow (0-60px blur)
