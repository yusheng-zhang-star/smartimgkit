🔒 The Privacy Difference That Matters

When you upload a photo to TinyPNG, that photo goes to their servers. For product shots, client work, or sensitive documents, that's a real concern. Squoosh and SmartImgKit both use WebAssembly to process everything locally — turn off your Wi-Fi and they still work. That's why privacy-focused developers increasingly prefer browser-native tools.

FAQ

Is TinyPNG still the best in 2026?

TinyPNG is still great for simple JPG/PNG compression, but it uploads files to its servers and limits free users to 20 images. Squoosh is better for privacy, and SmartImgKit offers the widest toolset.

Is Squoosh really free?

Yes, Squoosh by Google is completely free and open-source. It runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. However, it only compresses one image at a time.

Which compressor is most private?

Both Squoosh and SmartImgKit process images locally in your browser — files never leave your device. TinyPNG uploads every image to its cloud servers.
