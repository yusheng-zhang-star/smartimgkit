Etsy Product Photo Requirements 2026 (Exact Size & Tips)

Published: August 31, 2026 · 6 min read

On Etsy the thumbnail does the selling: shoppers judge a listing from the search grid without opening the description, so a soft or badly cropped photo loses the click no matter how good the product is. The usual culprit is small square phone photos — under 1200px on the longest side — which Etsy renders blurry and cropped. This guide covers the image sizes that work in 2026 and how to fix listings that get views but no clicks.

If your Etsy listings aren't getting clicks, 9 times out of 10 it's the photos. Etsy is a visual marketplace: shoppers scroll through search results and click based on the thumbnail. If your photos are the wrong size, cropped weird, or blurry, no one clicks, no matter how good your product is. Here's exactly what Etsy requires in 2026.

Why Your Etsy Photos Are Getting No Clicks

  • Wrong aspect ratio: If you upload a square photo, Etsy shows it small in search. 3:4 portrait photos take up more space in the search feed and get more clicks.
  • Blurry zoom: If your photo is under 2000px on the shortest side, Etsy turns off the zoom feature, and shoppers can't see product details.
  • Too few photos: Sellers with 10 photos have twice the conversion of sellers with 2 photos. Use all 10 slots: show the product from all angles, in use, on a model, with details, and size references.
  • Watermarks: Etsy discourages watermarks on product photos, and they make your listing look unprofessional. Remove any watermarks before uploading.
  • Text overlays: Big text on your main photo gets your listing flagged. Keep the main photo clean — just the product.

Best Practices That Actually Move the Needle

  • Use natural lighting: Etsy shoppers are looking for handmade, authentic products. Soft natural light on a neutral background works way better than studio flash.
  • Show the product in use: Don't just show the product on white. Show someone using it, holding it, wearing it. That helps shoppers imagine owning it.
  • Add a size reference: Include a photo with a common object next to the product (like a hand, a coffee mug) so shoppers can tell how big it actually is. Size confusion is the #1 reason for returns on Etsy.
  • Compress before upload: Etsy accepts up to 10MB, but files over 4MB take forever to upload on slow connections. Use our bulk compressor to get all your photos to 2-3MB JPG before uploading.

Common Mistakes New Sellers Make

  • Uploading WebP: Etsy doesn't accept WebP. You'll get an error. Use JPG.
  • Using PNG for photos: PNG files are huge. For regular product photos, JPG is 5x smaller. Only use PNG if you need a transparent background.
  • Not using all 10 slots: Every slot is an opportunity to show off your product. If you only have 2 photos, add lifestyle shots, detail shots, packaging shots.
  • Forgetting the thumbnail: The first photo is the one that shows up in search. Make it count: clean background, product centered, good lighting. Don't make the first photo a lifestyle shot that's too busy to tell what the product is.

Why 3:4 portrait beats square for Etsy search

You might be wondering why Etsy recommends 3:4 portrait when Amazon requires 1:1 square. It's because Etsy's users are 90% on mobile, and a 3:4 portrait image takes up 60% more vertical space on a phone screen than a square image. In the Etsy search feed, listings are stacked vertically — a tall portrait photo fills almost the whole screen width, while a square photo leaves big white bars on the left and right. Shoppers scrolling with their thumb are far more likely to stop and look at a tall, full-width image than a small square floating in a sea of whitespace. That's why top Etsy sellers all use 3:4: it's not a rule, it's a CTR optimization. It directly gets you more clicks from the same search position.
