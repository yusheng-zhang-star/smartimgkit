Etsy Product Photo Requirements: The 2026 Seller's Checklist

Published: 2026-09-19

Etsy listings with 5+ photos get 2x more sales than listings with just one. But every seller knows the pain: resizing, white-background conversion, and scene shots take hours. Here's the checklist.

The 5 Photos Every Etsy Listing Needs

  1. Main image (2000×2000, white background) — Etsy shows this in search results
  2. Close-up detail — shows texture and quality
  3. Lifestyle shot — product in use
  4. Detail/feature shot — highlights a selling point
  5. Packaging or size reference — builds trust

Why This Matters

Etsy's algorithm rewards complete listings. Sellers who skip the white-background main image or use low-resolution photos get filtered out of search. Photoshop or Canva can do this, but they're slow and require design skills.
