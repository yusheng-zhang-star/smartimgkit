2Canva — Best for Design Workflows

Since remove.bg is being integrated into Canva, it's a natural alternative. Canva's background remover works well and integrates with their massive library of templates, fonts, and design elements. However, full access requires Canva Pro at $12.99/month.

Step 1: Go to canva.com and create a free account (or log in if you already have one).
Step 2: Create a new design and upload your image by clicking "Uploads" → "Upload files."
Step 3: Click on your image to select it, then click "Edit photo" in the toolbar above.
Step 4: In the left panel, find and click "BG Remover." Canva will automatically remove the background.
Step 5: Use the "Restore" and "Erase" brushes to fine-tune the edges if needed.
Step 6: Download as PNG with transparent background. Note: free users get 50 removals/month with limited resolution.
💡 Tip: If you already use Canva for design work, this is the most convenient option. But if you only need background removal, a dedicated free tool like SmartImgKit saves you the subscription cost.
3Adobe Express — Best Output Quality

Adobe Express brings Photoshop-grade AI to a free browser tool. The background removal quality is among the best we've tested, especially for complex edges. Free users get 25 generative credits per month.

Step 1: Visit adobe.com/express and sign in with a free Adobe account.
Step 2: Upload your image and select it on the canvas.
Step 3: Click "Remove Background" in the image editing panel.
Step 4: Use the refinement brush to manually touch up any areas the AI missed.
Step 5: Download as PNG. Each removal uses one of your 25 monthly free credits.
4GIMP — Best Free Desktop Software

GIMP is a powerful, free, open-source photo editor that runs on Windows, Mac, and Linux. While it doesn't have one-click AI removal, its "Foreground Select" tool and "Fuzzy Select" tool can achieve excellent results with a bit of practice. Best of all, it's completely offline and private.

Step 1: Download and install GIMP from gimp.org (it's 100% free and open source).
Step 2: Open your image in GIMP via File → Open.
Step 3: Right-click the layer in the Layers panel and select "Add Alpha Channel" (this enables transparency).
Step 4: Select the "Foreground Select Tool" from the toolbox (it looks like a person with a dotted outline).
Step 5: Draw a rough selection around your subject, then paint over the foreground to mark it. Press Enter to convert to a selection.
Step 6: Go to Select → Invert, then press Delete to remove the background.
Step 7: Use the Eraser tool with a soft brush to clean up edges. Export as PNG via File → Export As.
5PowerPoint — Best for Quick & Simple

If you have Microsoft Office, PowerPoint has a built-in background removal tool that's surprisingly capable for simple images. It's not the best for complex edges like hair, but for product shots and simple graphics, it works well without any extra software.

Step 1: Open PowerPoint and insert your image via Insert → Pictures.
Step 2: Select the image, then click "Picture Format" → "Remove Background" (on Mac: "Format" → "Remove Background").
Step 3: PowerPoint will auto-detect the foreground. Use "Mark Areas to Keep" and "Mark Areas to Remove" tools to refine.
Step 4: Click "Keep Changes" to apply.
Step 5: Right-click the image → "Save as Picture" → choose PNG format to preserve transparency.
