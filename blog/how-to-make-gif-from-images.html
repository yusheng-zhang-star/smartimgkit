How to Make an Animated GIF from Images (Free, No Photoshop)

Published: 2026-09-20

GIFs are still the best format for short, silent, autoplaying loops. They're perfect for product demos, before/after comparisons, and reaction memes. But making one usually means opening Photoshop or paying for a subscription.

The Easier Way

Our GIF Maker does it all in your browser:

  1. Drop multiple images (order matters)
  2. Adjust frame delay (100-2000ms)
  3. Set output width (200-1200px)
  4. Click generate — done

It uses gif.js under the hood, so encoding happens locally. Your images never touch a server.

Why Not Use GIPHY or Imgur?

GIPHY and Imgur are great for hosting, but they require you to upload your images to their servers — a problem if you're making internal demos or prototypes. Our tool keeps everything on your device.
