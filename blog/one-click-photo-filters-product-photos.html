Filter Types by Product Category

👕 Apparel & Textiles

Use Studio White or Natural Bright. Avoid warm filters — they distort fabric colors. Consistency across all product photos is key.

📿 Jewelry & Accessories

Use Deep Contrast. Makes metal shine, gems sparkle, and details stand out on white backgrounds.

🍽️ Food & Kitchen

Use Food Warm. Adds warmth that makes food look fresh and appetizing — proven to increase click-through.

📱 Electronics & Tech

Use Cool Clean. Makes plastic and glass surfaces look crisp, reflections look professional.
