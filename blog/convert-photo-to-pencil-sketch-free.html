  1. Upload your photo: Click the upload area and select any JPG, PNG, or WebP image from your phone or computer. Portraits, pet photos, landscape shots, and product images all work well. For best results, pick a photo with clear, defined outlines — avoid super blurry or busy photos.
  2. Wait 1 second: The tool uses edge detection to identify outlines in your photo, automatically converting all solid color areas to pure white and all edges to clean black lines. No sliders, no adjustments needed.
  3. Download your sketch: Click the download button to save the black and white line art PNG directly to your device, no watermark, no size limit. The output resolution matches your original photo, so it's sharp enough to print.

Pro tips for better sketch results

  • Pick photos with clear subjects: A close-up portrait of a face, a pet sitting still, or a single product on a plain background converts perfectly. Busy landscape photos with lots of trees and texture will come out as messy scribbles.
  • Good lighting matters: Photos taken in bright, even light convert better than dark, shadowy photos — the edge detector can't find outlines in underexposed areas.
  • For coloring pages: Pick photos with simple shapes — avoid tiny details like jewelry or text on clothing, those will turn into tiny un-colorable lines.
