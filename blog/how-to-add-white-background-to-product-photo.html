Why White Background for Product Photos?

White background product photos are the standard for e-commerce. Amazon, eBay, Shopify, Walmart, and most other marketplaces require or strongly recommend pure white backgrounds (RGB 255,255,255) for product listings. White backgrounds eliminate distractions, make the product the sole focus, create a consistent shopping experience, and allow customers to clearly see product details, colors, and textures.

Traditionally, creating white background product photos required a professional studio with white seamless paper, softbox lighting, and expensive photo editing software. Today, you can achieve professional-quality white background product photos at home using a smartphone, natural light, and a free AI background remover tool. The SmartImgKit Product White Background tool automatically removes the original background and replaces it with pure white (RGB 255,255,255), meeting the strict requirements of Amazon and other platforms.

Platform Requirements

Different platforms have slightly different requirements for product photos. Here are the most common ones:

  • Amazon: Pure white background (RGB 255,255,255), product fills 85% of the image frame, minimum 1000px on the longest side (for zoom functionality), JPEG or TIFF format, no watermarks, no text, no logos (except the product itself).
  • eBay: White or light gray background recommended, minimum 500px on the longest side, JPEG format, no borders, no watermarks.
  • Shopify: White background recommended for consistency, square format (1:1 aspect ratio), minimum 800x800px, JPEG or PNG format.
  • Walmart: Pure white background required, product fills 85% of frame, minimum 1000px, JPEG format.
  • Etsy: White background recommended but not required, minimum 2000px on the shortest side, JPEG or PNG format.

Step-by-Step Tutorial

  1. Set up your shooting area. Find a well-lit area near a window with natural daylight. Hang a white sheet, poster board, or use a white wall as the background. Place your product on a white surface (table covered with white paper or cloth). The goal is to have even lighting with no harsh shadows.
  2. Take the photo. Use a smartphone or camera on a tripod (or stable surface). Position the product at eye level or slightly above. Make sure the product is in focus and well-lit. Take 3-5 photos from different angles and choose the best one. Avoid using flash (causes harsh shadows and reflections).

Pro Tips for Professional Results

💡

Diffuse the light

Direct sunlight creates harsh shadows. Hang a white sheet between the window and your product to diffuse the light, or shoot on an overcast day. Soft, diffused light produces the most professional-looking product photos.

📐

Fill 85% of frame

Amazon requires the product to fill 85% of the image frame. After removing the background, crop or zoom the image so the product takes up about 85% of the width and height. This makes the product appear larger and more prominent in search results.

🎨

Check color accuracy

Make sure the product colors are accurate in the photo. Natural daylight provides the most accurate color representation. Avoid fluorescent lighting (casts green tint) and incandescent lighting (casts yellow tint). If colors are off, use the Photo Enhancer tool to adjust white balance.

🔍

Check edges at 100%

After background removal, zoom to 100% and check the product edges for any remaining background color, halo artifacts, or jagged edges. The AI model handles most edges well, but fine details like hair, fur, or transparent materials may need manual refinement.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using a colored or patterned background. Even if you plan to remove the background later, starting with a white or light-colored background gives the AI more contrast and produces cleaner edges. Avoid busy patterns or dark backgrounds.
  • Harsh shadows. Shadows can make it difficult for the AI to distinguish the product from the background. Use diffused lighting and position the product slightly away from the background to minimize shadows. If shadows remain, the AI will remove them along with the background.
  • Low resolution. Amazon requires a minimum of 1000px on the longest side for zoom functionality. Take photos at the highest resolution your camera supports. You can always downsize later, but you cannot add detail that was never captured.
  • Not checking for pure white. Some "white" backgrounds are actually off-white or light gray. Amazon requires pure white (RGB 255,255,255). The Product White Background tool ensures the background is exactly pure white, but always verify with a color picker if you are unsure.
  • Using watermarks or text. Amazon and most platforms prohibit watermarks, text, or logos on product photos (except the product itself). Do not add your logo, website URL, or any promotional text to the main product photo.
