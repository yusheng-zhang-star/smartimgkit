How to Convert PDF Pages to Images for Free (2026)

Published: 2026-09-21

Need to turn a PDF into images? Maybe you want to insert a slide into a PowerPoint, share a page on social media, or extract figures from a research paper. The usual tools are Adobe Acrobat (paid) or online converters that upload your file to a server.

The Privacy Problem

Most PDF-to-image converters upload your file to their server, convert it there, and let you download it. That's fine for public documents, but risky if your PDF contains contracts, medical records, or internal reports.

Local Conversion with pdf.js

Our PDF to Images workflow uses pdf.js (the same engine Firefox uses) to render every page directly in your browser. You get:

  • 1-4x scale control (crisp or small)
  • PNG or JPEG output
  • All pages packaged as a ZIP
  • Zero upload — the PDF never leaves your device

It's also available as a standalone tool at pdf.smartimgkit.com/pdf-to-image.
