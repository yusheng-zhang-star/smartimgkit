Remove EXIF Data from Photos Before Sharing (Privacy Guide)

Published: 2026-09-26

Every photo you take with a smartphone contains hidden metadata: GPS coordinates, camera model, exact time, and even your phone's serial number. Post that photo online and anyone can extract that data.

What EXIF Data Reveals

  • GPS location — where you took the photo (often your home)
  • Camera/phone model — what device you use
  • Date and time — when you took it
  • Software — what edits you applied

How to Clean Photos

Social media platforms like Instagram and Facebook strip EXIF automatically when you upload through their apps. But if you share a photo directly (via email, Discord, or a blog), the metadata stays. Our EXIF Remover re-encodes your photos through canvas, stripping all metadata in one click.
