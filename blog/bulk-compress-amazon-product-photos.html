How to Compress Bulk Product Photos for Amazon Listings

Published October 5, 2026 · 3 min read

Yes, you can bulk compress product photos for Amazon listings locally in your browser without uploading them to a server. Amazon recommends images under 10MB for faster page loading, and heavy images slow down your listing and reduce conversion.

✅ Local Browser Compression

  • Images never leave your device
  • Unlimited files per batch
  • No signup required
  • Adjust quality slider in real time

❌ Cloud Compression Tools

  • Upload photos to third-party servers
  • Free batches limited to 10-20 files
  • Require email signup for ZIP download
  • Wait for slow upload times

Step-by-Step Amazon Image Compression

  1. Open the Bulk Image Resizer
  2. Drag all your product photos into the upload zone
  3. Select "Amazon 2000px" preset, choose JPG at 80% quality
  4. Process all images, check file sizes are under 10MB
  5. Download and upload directly to Amazon Seller Central
