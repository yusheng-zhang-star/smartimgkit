Amazon's Exact Image Requirements

  • Format: JPEG (preferred), PNG, TIFF, GIF. No WebP.
  • File size: Max 10MB per image. We recommend targeting 1-4MB for fastest upload.
  • Resolution: Minimum 1000×1000px for zoom, recommended 2000×2000px for best quality. Max 10,000×10,000px.
  • Color mode: sRGB. Don't upload Adobe RGB photos — Amazon converts them and the colors look washed out.
  • Background: Pure white for main images (Amazon's strict requirement).
The most common rejection reason is file size over 10MB. A raw JPG from a modern phone is 8-15MB, which is already over the limit for most cameras.

Step-by-Step Bulk Compression Workflow

  1. Select all your product photos in our bulk compressor. You can upload up to 100 at a time.
  2. Set compression quality to 75% — that's the sweet spot for Amazon: file size drops by 70% with no visible quality loss on product photos.
  3. Check "resize to 2000×2000px" — this automatically resizes any larger images down to the recommended Amazon size, which makes the files even smaller.
  4. Click compress. All files process locally in your browser, then download as a ZIP.
  5. Upload the compressed JPGs directly to Amazon Seller Central — every file will be under 4MB, 2000×2000px, and meet all Amazon requirements.
Your original product photos never get uploaded to any server. This is especially important if you have unreleased products — no one else can access your shots.

Why Compress Locally Instead of Using Amazon's Built-In Compression?

Amazon does compress images after you upload them, but:

  • If your original file is over 10MB, Amazon rejects it outright before it can compress it.
  • Amazon's compression is inconsistent — sometimes it leaves files at 8MB, sometimes it squashes them too much and they look blurry.
  • Uploading 20MB files takes forever. At 4MB per file, uploads are 5x faster. If you have 100 listings, that's hours saved.
Compressing before you upload also means you control the quality — you can make sure your product photos look sharp, not whatever Amazon's compression decides to do.

Common Mistakes

  • Compressing too much: If you set quality below 60%, your product photos will look pixelated when customers zoom in. Stick to 70-80% quality for Amazon.
  • Not resizing before compressing: A 4000×4000 photo compressed to 2MB is still 4000×4000px, which is way bigger than Amazon needs. Resize to 2000×2000 first, then compress — that cuts file size by another 50%.
  • Uploading PNGs for product photos: PNG files are 5-10x bigger than JPGs for the same product photo. Use JPG for regular product shots, only use PNG if you need a transparent background.
  • Forgetting sRGB color mode: If you shoot in RAW and export in Adobe RGB, the colors will look off on Amazon. Export as sRGB JPG.
