Passport Photo Background Changer: Swap to White, Blue, or Red in Seconds

A passport photo background changer is a free online tool that removes the original background from your ID photo and replaces it with a solid color — white, light blue, or red — so your picture meets embassy and government requirements without leaving your house.

Which Background Color Do You Need?

Not every country uses the same background. Getting this wrong means your passport application gets rejected. Here is the quick reference:

WHITE

US / UK / Schengen

Plain white or off-white. No shadows. Most common requirement worldwide.

LIGHT BLUE

China / India / Thailand

Medium light blue (#438EDB). Required for Chinese passports and Indian visa photos.

RED

Some ID Cards

Red is used for national ID cards in China and a few other countries, not for passports.

Using the wrong color wastes your application fee and adds weeks of delay. Always double-check your destination country's official requirements before you print.

How to Change Your Passport Photo Background in 3 Steps

  1. Upload your photo. Take a selfie against any wall and upload it as JPG or PNG. No studio needed.

Tips for a Passport-Ready Result

Face the camera directly. Look straight at the lens. No tilted heads, no sunglasses, no hats (religious headwear excepted).

Use even lighting. Stand facing a window. Harsh side light creates shadows the AI cannot fully remove.

Wear everyday clothes. Uniforms and camouflage are rejected. Casual clothing works fine.

Keep shoulders in frame. The AI cuts out your upper body automatically. Crop too tight and the edges look unnatural.
