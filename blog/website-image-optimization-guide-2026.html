How to Optimize Images for Web in 2026: A Developer's Guide

September 17, 2026 · 8 min read

If your website loads slowly, images are almost always the culprit. The average web page weighs over 2 MB, and images make up 65% of that weight. Here's how to fix it in 2026.

Why image optimization still matters

Google's Core Web Vitals treat LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) as a ranking factor. Images that are too large directly hurt your LCP score, your SEO, and your conversion rate. Even a 1-second delay can cost 7% of conversions.

The 3-step optimization workflow

  1. Resize before you compress. A 4000×3000 photo from your camera doesn't need to be 4000×3000 on the web. Cap width at 1920px for hero images, 1200px for blog posts.
  2. Convert to WebP. WebP is 25-35% smaller than JPEG at identical visual quality. Browser support is at 97% globally.
  3. Compress intelligently. 80% quality is the sweet spot — invisible to the eye but 50-70% smaller than the original.
