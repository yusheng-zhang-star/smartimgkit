Make Custom Phone Wallpapers From Your Own Photos (Free)

September 21, 2026 · 4 min read

That sunset photo you took on vacation? Your kid's drawing? The AI art you generated? It's sitting in your camera roll. Make it your wallpaper — but don't just save it straight to your phone. Phone screens are tall and narrow; a landscape photo will get awkwardly cropped. Here's the right way.

Lock screen vs home screen

Your lock screen shows the clock and notifications. Your home screen shows app icons. They need different treatment: the lock version benefits from a dark gradient at the top so the clock stays readable. The home version can be the full image.

Wallpaper apps vs this tool

Walli, Zedge, and similar apps give you wallpapers made by others. Great if you want that, but they don't use your photos. They also require downloads, show ads, and collect your data.

The Phone Wallpaper Maker does something different: it takes your photo and auto-crops it to every phone screen size that matters — iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPhone 15, iPhone SE, and Android. It generates both lock screen (with the gradient) and home screen versions. All in your browser, free, no upload.

Which photos work best

  • Landscape photos — choose "bottom" focus so the sky stays visible
  • Portrait photos — center focus works well
  • Abstract or gradient art — any focus, looks great
  • Text or logos — avoid; they'll get cut off

How to use

  1. Upload your photo
  2. Choose "All phones" to get a ZIP with every size
  3. Pick lock + home screen versions
  4. Generate, download, set as wallpaper on your phone

Bonus: share your wallpapers

Custom wallpapers are highly shareable. If you make one from a beautiful photo, post it on your social media with the hashtag. It's a low-effort way to get likes and follows.

FAQ

What about iPad? For tablets, use the Resizer with custom dimensions. This tool targets phones.

Will the gradient bother me? It's subtle — just enough contrast for the clock to read. You can also choose "home only" for a clean full image.
