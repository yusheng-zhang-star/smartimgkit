WebP vs JPG: Which Is Better for Online Store Listings?

Published October 5, 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer: use WebP for your own Shopify store for 30% smaller file sizes, use JPG for Amazon/Etsy main images because they require it.

Is WebP better than JPG for ecommerce?
Yes. WebP gives 25-35% smaller file size at identical visual quality, which means faster store loading, better SEO rankings, and less bandwidth for your customers on mobile data.
Do Amazon and Etsy support WebP?
Most marketplaces still require JPG for main listing images. But you can convert your WebP files to JPG in one click in our batch tool when you need to upload to marketplaces.
