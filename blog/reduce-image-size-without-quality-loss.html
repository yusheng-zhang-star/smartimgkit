How to Reduce Image File Size Without Losing Quality

Published October 5, 2026 · 3 min read

Yes, you can cut image file sizes by 50-70% with no visible quality loss. Follow these simple steps for product photos that look identical but load much faster.

Step 1: Resize first

Never upload a 4000x3000 camera photo to your store. Resize down to 2000px on the longest side — this alone cuts file size by 75% with zero quality loss.

Step 2: Set quality to 80-85%

JPEG compression at 80% quality is indistinguishable from 100% to the human eye, but cuts file size by half. Drop below 70% and you will start seeing visible artifacts.

Step 3: Convert to WebP

WebP compression gives another 25% size reduction on top of JPEG, at identical visual quality. Use WebP for your own store, JPG for marketplace uploads.
