Why Pixelate Images Instead of Just Blurring Them?

Pixelation turns sensitive content into an unrecognizable grid of colored blocks, which is far more secure than a simple Gaussian blur. Unlike blur effects that can sometimes be reversed with AI restoration, pixelation permanently obscures the content so it can never be recovered.

This is why pixelation is the standard for journalists, bloggers, legal teams, and anyone publishing photos publicly. It ensures you never accidentally reveal private information in screenshots or photos you share online.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can pixelated images be un-pixelated?

When you use a high enough pixel block size, the original detail is permanently destroyed and cannot be recovered. Lower pixel sizes may be partially reversible with AI tools, so use 8px+ blocks for sensitive content.

Does this tool add watermarks?

Yes, the built-in undo button lets you revert your last strokes before you download the final image.
