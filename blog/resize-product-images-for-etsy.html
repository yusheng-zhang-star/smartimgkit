Etsy Image Requirements You Must Follow

Etsy ranks listings with high-resolution photos higher in search. The official requirements are: longest side at least 2000px, file size under 10MB, main image square 1:1 aspect ratio. Photos smaller than 2000px lose the zoom feature, which reduces conversion by up to 20% per Etsy's own seller data.

Why You Should Use a Local Batch Resizer

Most online resizers (like iLoveIMG) upload your product photos to their servers. This creates privacy risk for original designs, and they limit free batches to 10 or 20 images. A local browser tool never sends your photos anywhere, and processes unlimited batches for free.
