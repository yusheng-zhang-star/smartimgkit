Etsy image requirements that actually affect sales

Etsy's official photo rules sound simple on the surface, but they have a huge impact on how many people buy from you:

  • Longest side must be at least 2000px. Any smaller and Etsy disables the zoom feature on your listing. Seller data consistently shows that listings with zoom enabled get 18-22% more sales — buyers want to see fabric texture, engravings, print quality before they click buy.
  • File size must be under 10MB. Phone photos straight from a modern iPhone are often 3-5MB as HEIC, which Etsy doesn't even accept. Resizing to JPG at 85% quality brings that down to 300-500KB, which loads instantly on mobile search.
  • Main image should be square 1:1. Etsy search results on mobile crop images to a square. If your photo is 4:3 portrait, the top and bottom get cut off — you might lose the key detail that makes someone click.
  • You can upload up to 10 photos per listing. Use all 10: show the product from every angle, lifestyle shots, scale references, close-ups of details. Each extra photo increases conversion.

Why uploading straight from your phone is costing you sales

Most new Etsy sellers just snap a product photo on their phone and upload it directly. This fails for three reasons:

  1. Phone photos are often too tall. A default iPhone photo is 4032×3024px (4:3), which Etsy crops awkwardly in search results. You need to crop it to a clean square, centered on your product.
  2. File sizes are huge. A raw iPhone HEIC photo can be 8MB, which Etsy rejects outright. You have to convert it to JPG and compress it without visible quality loss.
  3. You're processing one photo at a time. If you're listing 10 new products this week, that's 50 photos you'd have to manually export one by one in Photoshop. That's an hour of work you could spend making products.

Small tweaks that make your Etsy images convert better

  • Center your product in the square crop. Don't leave the product in the corner with empty space. The resizer centers automatically, but double-check that important details (like a handmade logo tag) aren't getting cropped out.
  • Keep the first photo clean. The main listing photo should show your product clearly on a simple background. Save the lifestyle shots (mug on a desk, jewelry on a model) for photos 2-5.
  • Don't over-compress. 85% JPG quality is fine — going lower to 60% makes fabric and wood textures look fuzzy, which makes buyers think the product is low quality.
  • Keep all photos the same aspect ratio. If every photo in your listing is 2000×2000 square, the Etsy image gallery looks clean and professional. Mixed sizes make your shop look amateur.
