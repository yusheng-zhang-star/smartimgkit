Why Amazon Strictly Enforces Square White Background Images

Amazon automatically suppresses product listings that don't meet image requirements, which means your product will not show up in search results or on product detail pages until your images are fixed. The current Amazon main image requirements are non-negotiable: images must be 1:1 aspect ratio, minimum 1000x1000 pixels, pure white background (RGB 255,255,255), the product must fill at least 85% of the frame, and no text, logos, or watermarks are allowed on the main listing image.

For most new e-commerce sellers, the hardest part is getting the product perfectly centered on a white background at exactly 2000x2000 resolution. Previously, sellers had to pay a photographer, or spend 1.99 dollars a month on Photoshop, manually cropping and padding every product photo. This tool eliminates that entire workflow.
