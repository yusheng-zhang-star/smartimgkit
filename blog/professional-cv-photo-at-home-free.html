What Makes a CV Photo Look Professional?

A standard professional CV photo requires: 2x2 inch (or 35x45mm) size, plain white/light gray background, neutral facial expression, even lighting, business casual clothing, and the head takes up 60-70% of the frame. Most people mess this up at home: bad lighting, messy backgrounds, wrong size, awkward angles.

Step-by-Step: Take Your CV Photo at Home (5 Minutes Total)

  1. Stand 1.5 meters from a plain white wall, use natural daylight from a window to avoid harsh shadows on your face.
  2. Have a friend hold your phone at eye level, take 5-10 shots, pick the one where you look straight at the camera.
  3. Upload your best shot to our local ID photo tool: it automatically cuts out your background and replaces it with pure white.
  4. Adjust the crop to standard CV size, center your face, and export as high-res JPG.
  5. Use the photo on your resume, LinkedIn profile, and job application portals.
