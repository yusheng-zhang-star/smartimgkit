Tips for Better Social Images

  • Use a square original image: If your original product photo is square, the tool can crop it to any aspect ratio without cutting off important parts. If your original is landscape, portrait crops will cut off the sides.
  • Center your product: Make sure the product is in the center of the original image. When the tool crops to different aspect ratios, it will center on the middle of the image.
  • Add text for Pinterest: Pinterest pins with text overlay get 2x more clicks. After resizing, add your product name and price as text on the portrait version.
  • Compress after resizing: Social media platforms compress images anyway, so keep your files under 1MB for faster uploads. Use our compressor after resizing.

Common Mistakes

  • Posting the same image to every platform: If you upload a square Instagram post to Pinterest, it will show up as a tiny square in the feed, no one will click on it. Resize for each platform.
  • Cutting off important parts: If your product is on the left side of the original image, portrait crops will cut it off. Adjust the crop manually for each platform.
  • Using low-resolution images: All platforms require at least 1080px on the long edge. If your original image is too small, it will look pixelated when uploaded.

Instagram users respond best to clean square or 9:16 story images that feel authentic and casual, like real life, not a sales ad. LinkedIn and Facebook users respond best to wide landscape images that look professional and clean, because those platforms are used by people in a more work-oriented mindset. If you leave 10-15% empty space around the product when you take the original shot, the resizer has room to crop to any aspect ratio without cutting into your product, and you won't have to manually adjust every crop.
