Tips for Better Batch Results

  • Shoot on a consistent background: If all your product photos are shot on the same white sheet, the automatic background removal will work perfectly for all of them. If you have a mix of backgrounds, you might need to manually touch up a few edges.
  • Process in batches of 20: If you have 200 photos, process them in batches of 20 instead of all at once. It uses less memory on your computer, and it's easier to catch errors early.
  • Check a few after processing: Don't just upload all 50 to your store without checking. Open 2-3 random ones and zoom in on the edges to make sure the cutout is clean. If a few have rough edges, you can fix them in Preview or any free editor.
  • Save as PNG: Transparent backgrounds only work in PNG format. The batch tool automatically exports as PNG, so you don't have to change anything.

Common Mistakes

  • Trying to process 500 photos at once: Your browser will run out of memory and crash. Process in batches of 20-50.
  • Not checking edges after processing: Automatic cutouts are 95% perfect, but the last 5% of edges might need a quick touch-up. It's worth checking before you upload to your store.
  • Using online batch tools that upload your photos: Most free batch tools upload all your photos to their server. Our tool runs locally, so your photos never leave your computer.
  • Forgetting to put products on white background: Amazon requires a pure white main image, not transparent. After removing the background, you need to put the cutout on a solid white background before uploading to Amazon.
