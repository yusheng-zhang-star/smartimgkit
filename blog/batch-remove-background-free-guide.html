Why Browser-Based Batch Is Better Than Cloud APIs

Traditional batch background removers send your photos to a cloud server. Each upload consumes bandwidth. If you have a slow connection, 50 images can take 10 minutes just to upload before processing even starts.

SmartImgKit uses WebAssembly (ONNX Runtime Web) to run the AI model directly in your browser. There is zero upload. The model runs on your device's GPU or CPU. The trade-off is speed depends on your computer, but there is no server cost — which is why the tool can be 100% free.

Privacy: Why It Matters for Product Photos

Many sellers have unreleased products, confidential client work, or private photos they cannot risk uploading to a third-party server. Browser-based processing means the images never leave your device. No logs, no training data usage, no data sharing.

This is not just a marketing claim. Open your browser's network tab while processing — you will see zero image data being sent out. The model downloads once, then everything runs locally.

How the AI Model Works in Your Browser

SmartImgKit uses the ISNet (Image Segmentation Network) model via ONNX Runtime Web. This is the same family of models that powers commercial background removal APIs — but it runs locally on your device.

The model automatically detects the main subject — person, product, pet, or object — and separates it from the background. It handles fine details like hair strands, transparent objects, and complex edges that older tools struggle with.

Device selection is automatic: WebGPU if your browser supports it, WebGL as fallback, WASM (CPU) as last resort. You don't need to configure anything.

Common Questions About Batch Processing Speed

How long does 50 images take? On a modern laptop with GPU acceleration, expect about 5–8 seconds per image. That's 4–7 minutes total for 50 photos. On an older CPU-only machine, plan for 15–20 seconds per image.

Why is the first batch slow? The first time you use the tool, it downloads the ~40MB ISNet model. After that, the browser caches it locally and processing starts immediately. You only re-download if you clear your browser cache.

Is Batch Background Removal Accurate?

The ISNet model is designed for general-purpose image segmentation. It handles most product photos and portraits well. For complex backgrounds — forests, busy streets, or fine hair details — you may get minor edge artifacts. For simple studio shots or white-background products, the result is production-ready.
