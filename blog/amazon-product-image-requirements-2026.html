Amazon Product Image Requirements 2026: What Every Seller Must Know

September 18, 2026 · 6 min read

Have you ever had a listing suppressed because your images didn't meet Amazon's rules? It happens more than you think. Here's the complete 2026 checklist.

The rules that get listings suppressed

  • Main image background must be pure white (RGB 255,255,255). No props, no scene, no color.
  • Minimum 1000×1000 pixels for zoom functionality. Amazon recommends 2000×2000.
  • Product must fill 85% of the frame. No excessive white space.
  • No text, logos, or watermarks on the main image.
  • No borders on the main image.
  • JPEG format preferred (PNG and GIF also accepted).

Main image vs secondary images

The main image follows strict rules. Secondary images (positions 2-7) are where you sell: show the product in use, dimensions, scale, details, and packaging. Smart sellers use secondary images to answer buyer questions before they ask.

How long does professional product photography cost?
