US Passport Photo Requirements 2026: Complete Size, Background & Rules Guide

Published October 2, 2026 · 6 min read

US passport photos must be exactly 2x2 inches (51x51 mm), taken against a plain white background, with your head centered and measuring 1 to 1 3/8 inches from chin to crown. These requirements are set by the U.S. Department of State and must be followed exactly — even small deviations can cause your application to be rejected.

Photo Size and Dimensions

The US passport photo has a strict square format. Both printed and digital photos must meet these exact measurements.

MeasurementRequirement
Overall size2 x 2 inches (51 x 51 mm)
Head height (chin to crown)1 to 1 3/8 inches (25 to 35 mm)

Digital resolution600 x 600 pixels minimum

Background Requirements

The background must be plain white or off-white. No patterns, textures, shadows, or objects are allowed behind you. The background must be evenly lit — any dark areas or shadows behind your head will cause rejection.

Head Position and Framing

  • Face the camera directly with your head centered in the frame
  • Both ears must be visible
  • Eyes must be open and looking straight at the camera
  • No head tilt — keep your head level
  • Do not crop the top of your head

Facial Expression

You must have a neutral expression. Both eyes must be open. A slight natural smile is acceptable, but showing your teeth is not allowed. Your mouth must be closed naturally.

Glasses and Head Coverings

Glasses are no longer acceptable in US passport photos (since 2016), even if you wear them daily. The only exception is if you have a medical condition that requires them — in that case you need a signed doctor's statement. Sunglasses and tinted lenses are always prohibited.

Head coverings are allowed only for religious or medical reasons. The cover must not cast any shadows on your face, and your full face must still be visible from hairline to chin.

Lighting and Exposure

  • Even lighting across your entire face
  • No harsh shadows under your chin or on the background
  • No red-eye effect
  • No overexposure or underexposure
  • Color must be natural — no filters or color correction

Clothing and Dress Code

Wear what you normally wear daily. Uniforms or camouflage clothing are not allowed, except for religious attire. Do not wear hats or headbands unless required for religious reasons. Avoid white or very light-colored tops that blend into the background.
