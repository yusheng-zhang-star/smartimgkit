What TinyPNG doesn't tell you on its homepage

1. All photos are uploaded to the cloud: TinyPNG runs entirely on remote servers. Every photo you drag in gets sent over the internet to their servers, compressed there, then sent back to you. Unreleased product shots, private ID photos and sensitive images are exposed during this upload window.

2. 5MB per file limit: Free TinyPNG only accepts images up to 5MB. Modern iPhone photos and professional product renders are often 10-20MB, which gets rejected immediately on the free tier.

3. Batch cap: Free users can only upload 20 images per batch. If you have 100 product photos to compress, you have to split them into 5 separate uploads. Paid plans start at $3 per 500 compressions.

4. Fixed lossy compression: TinyPNG uses a one-size-fits-all lossy algorithm. There is no slider to adjust how aggressive the compression is, and it permanently discards color detail even when you would rather keep quality.

5. Images stored for 24 hours: TinyPNG keeps every uploaded photo on its servers for 24 hours before deletion. There is no way to request immediate deletion of your specific files.
