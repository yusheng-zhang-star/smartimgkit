When you're taking the original photos to process, you don't need a fancy studio setup — but there are a couple of quick tips that make the AI cutout way cleaner, which saves you from having to manually fix messy edges. First, make sure your product is well-lit from the front, so there's a clear contrast between the product and whatever background you're shooting on. If you take the photo in a dark room with weird backlighting, the AI will struggle to tell where the product ends and the background begins, especially on fuzzy items like plush toys, knit fabrics, or thin jewelry. Second, don't take the photo at a crazy angle — shoot straight on, or at a slight 45-degree angle, the same way you'd want it to appear in your listing. The AI can cut out almost any product, but it does better when the product is clearly in frame, not half-cropped or tilted sideways. Third, make sure your product isn't the same color as the background you're shooting on — don't take a white mug photo against a white wall, because the AI will have a hard time finding the edge. Shoot it against a contrasting surface like a wooden table, a dark countertop, or even a sheet of colored paper, and the cutout will be perfectly clean in one try. These three tiny tips make a massive difference in how good your final white-background photos look, and they take no extra effort when you're snapping the original shot.
