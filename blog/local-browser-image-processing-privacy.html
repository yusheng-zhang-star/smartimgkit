Why You Should Process Images Locally in Your Browser

Published October 5, 2026 · 3 min read

Last year, a freelance photographer uploaded 200 client wedding photos to a free online resizer to shrink them for delivery. A week later, the resizer was acquired by a large advertising company, and the terms of service updated to allow them to use uploaded images for marketing. None of those wedding photos were ever intended to be public.

That is why local browser image processing is the future. When an image tool runs entirely in your browser, every resize, compression and edit happens on your own device. Your photos are never uploaded to a server, never stored in a database, and never accessible to anyone else.

The Hidden Risk of Cloud Image Tools

Every popular online image editor (iLoveIMG, ResizePixel, Canva free tier) uploads your photos to their servers to process them. This means:

  • Your images are stored on third-party servers, subject to data breaches
  • The company can change their terms of service at any time to claim rights to your photos
  • Uploading large photos takes time, especially on slow internet connections
