Why Removing Text Used to Be So Hard

Before AI, removing text from an image meant manually cloning, healing, and painting over each letter one by one. If the background was complex — grass, fabric, skin, textured walls — it was basically impossible to make it look natural. Even professional designers would spend 30+ minutes on a single watermark.

Modern AI inpainting tools like LaMa and Stable Diffusion Inpaint changed everything. They understand what the background should look like and regenerate it seamlessly. The result? You paint over the text, click a button, and it's gone — like it was never there.

Tips for Best Results

Use high-resolution images

The AI needs enough pixel detail to understand what the background should look like. Low-res images will produce blurry results. Start with the highest quality version you have.

Remove text first, then edit

If you're doing multiple edits — background removal, resizing, filters — remove the text first. If you do it last, the AI might mess up other parts of the image you already edited.

Use a slightly larger brush

Don't trace the exact outline of each letter. Paint a little bit around the text as well. The AI needs context from the surrounding area to fill in properly. If you only cover the exact pixels of the text, the result might look patchy.

For complex backgrounds, do it in layers

If the text is on a complex background (like a crowd, trees, or fabric), break it into smaller sections. Remove one word at a time, checking the result after each pass. This gives the AI the best chance of matching the background perfectly.

FAQ: Removing Text from Images

Is it legal to remove text from images?

It depends on the image. You can remove text from your own photos freely. For copyrighted images, removing watermarks or copyright notices may violate the DMCA. Always make sure you have the right to edit the image before removing text.

Can I remove handwritten text from photos?

Yes! AI inpainting tools work just as well on handwritten text as they do on printed text. The process is exactly the same — paint over the handwriting and let the AI fill it in.

Why does my result look blurry?

Most likely, the original image was low resolution. Try using a higher quality version. If that doesn't work, try a second pass — paint over the blurry area again and let the AI refine it.

Can I remove text from a screenshot?

Absolutely. Screenshots are actually one of the most common use cases. Whether it's a username, a caption, or a UI element you want to hide, AI inpainting tools handle screenshots perfectly. Just remember that screenshot resolution is limited by your screen, so results won't be as sharp as a high-res photo.
