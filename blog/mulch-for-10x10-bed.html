How Much Mulch Do I Need for a 10x10 Bed? (2026 Chart)

Direct answer: For a standard 10ft x 10ft garden bed at 2 inches deep, you need 9 standard 1.5 cubic-foot bags of mulch (13.5 cubic feet total). If you want 3 inches of mulch for better weed control, buy 13 bags.

How to Calculate Mulch for Any Bed

  1. Measure the length and width of your bed in feet
  2. Multiply length × width to get square footage
  3. Multiply square footage by desired depth (2 inches = 0.17ft, 3 inches = 0.25ft)
  4. Divide by 1.5 to get number of standard bags
  5. Add 10% extra for settling and uneven ground

How Much Will It Cost?

Bagged hardwood mulch typically costs $3.50-$5 per 1.5 cubic foot bag. That means your 10x10 bed will cost $31.50-$45 for 9 bags. If you buy bulk mulch delivered, it costs $35-$60 per cubic yard, and you'll need less than half a yard for a 10x10 bed—bulk is cheaper for beds larger than 200 sq ft.

FAQ

How many bags of mulch for 10x10 bed?
You need 9 standard 1.5 cubic foot bags for a 10x10 bed at 2 inches deep, or 13 bags at 3 inches deep.
How much does mulch cost?
Bulk mulch costs $30-$60 per cubic yard, while bagged mulch costs $3-$5 per 1.5 cubic foot bag.
Is 2 inches of mulch enough?
2 inches is enough for established gardens and winter protection. For new beds and heavy weed areas, use 3 inches for better weed suppression.
