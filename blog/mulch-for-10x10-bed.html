How Much Mulch Do I Need for a 10x10 Bed?

Direct answer: You need 9 standard 1.5 cubic-foot bags of mulch (13.5 cubic feet total) for a 10x10 foot garden bed, spread 2 inches deep. If you want 3 inches deep, buy 13 bags.

That's the number most homeowners search for every spring. The math is simple: 10ft x 10ft = 100 sq ft. At 2 inches deep, you need 16.7 cubic feet, minus 20% for settling = 13.5 cubic feet.

10x10 bed, 2 inches deep → 9 bags (1.5 cu ft each)
10x10 bed, 3 inches deep → 13 bags
