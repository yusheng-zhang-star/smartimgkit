Why Cloud Batch Compressors Are Not Good for Sellers

Hidden limits

TinyPNG free plan only lets you compress 50 images per month. iLoveIMG free plan caps at 30 images per day. If you have 100 new product photos to process, you have to wait 3 days to finish.

Privacy risk

Every photo you upload goes to their cloud servers. For unreleased product shots, that means your new catalog could leak before launch.

Hidden quality loss

Most cloud compressors silently cut resolution by 50% to save space, and you don't notice until Amazon rejects your listing for low-res images.

No batch ZIP download

You have to download every compressed image one by one, which takes 10+ minutes for a batch of 50.
