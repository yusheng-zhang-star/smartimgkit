Best settings for common use cases

For Discord/Twitch avatars: 15px white outline, 30% corner roundness, add a 5px soft shadow under the sticker — this is the exact look most streamers use. For Instagram profile pictures: 10px white outline, minimal roundness, so your face stays the focus and the outline just lifts you off the default profile background. For custom merch/sticker printing: 20px outline, full rounded corners, add a 1px bleed outline around the color border so it prints cleanly.
