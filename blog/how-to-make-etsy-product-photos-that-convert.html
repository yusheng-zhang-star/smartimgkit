The 4 Elements of a High-Converting Etsy Product Photo

1. Clean White Background

White-background photos get 15-25% more clicks than busy lifestyle shots. They let the product speak for itself and match Etsy's clean aesthetic.

2. Realistic Contact Shadow

A product floating on white looks fake. A soft contact shadow under the object makes it look grounded and professional, increasing perceived value.

3. Correct 2000x2000 Size

Etsy recommends 2000x2000 pixels. This enables the zoom feature, which buyers use 40% of the time to inspect product details.

4. Good Lighting

Shoot in natural daylight near a window. Avoid harsh overhead flash. Even, soft light shows product texture and color accurately.

How Many Photos Should You List on Etsy?

Etsy allows up to 10 photos per listing. But data shows that listings with 5-7 photos perform best.

Photo 1 should be the clean white-background hero shot. This is what shows up in search results and thumbnails.

Photo 2 should show the product in use or in context. This helps buyers imagine owning it.

Photo 3 should show a close-up detail shot. Zoom in on texture, stitching, or craftsmanship.

Photos 4-7 can show scale, packaging, variations, and lifestyle scenes. More photos reduce buyer uncertainty and lower return rates.

Sellers who use all 7 photo slots see a 12-18% higher conversion rate than those who list only 1-2 photos.
