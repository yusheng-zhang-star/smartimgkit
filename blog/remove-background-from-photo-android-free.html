How to Remove the Background from Any Photo on Android (Free, No App)

Whether you're on a Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, Xiaomi, or any Android phone, here are four ways to erase photo backgrounds — without paying for an app.

Why Android Users Needed a Better Solution

iPhone users have had a one-tap "Remove Background" option in Photos since iOS 16. Android doesn't have a universal equivalent — Samsung hides Object Eraser deep in Gallery settings, Google Pixel relies on Magic Eraser (which removes objects, not backgrounds), and budget phones from Xiaomi, Oppo, and Motorola offer nothing at all.

The good news: Chrome on Android is powerful enough to run AI background removal directly in the browser. No app install, no subscription, no storage bloat. Here's how.

Method 1: Browser-Based Removal (Fastest, Free)

This is the best method for almost everyone. It works on every Android phone and costs nothing.

1

Open Chrome and go to SmartImgKit

Launch Chrome (or Firefox, Edge, Brave) and navigate to the SmartImgKit background remover. It loads in about a second, even on slower phones.

2

Tap "Upload" and pick your photo

Chrome will open your Android photo picker. Select any JPG, PNG, or WebP. The file never leaves your phone — processing runs locally.

3

Wait 3–5 seconds

On a mid-range Android phone, the AI model processes the image in about 4 seconds. Older devices may take 8–10 seconds.

4

Download the transparent PNG

Tap download. The PNG saves to your Downloads folder. Open it in any app that supports transparency — Instagram stories, Canva, WhatsApp stickers, or your e-commerce app.

💡 Pro tip: Add SmartImgKit to your home screen. In Chrome, tap the three-dot menu and select "Add to Home screen." It works like an app but doesn't take up 50MB of storage.

Method 2: Samsung Galaxy Object Eraser

If you have a recent Samsung (S21 and newer, Z Fold/Flip series), the Gallery app has a built-in tool:

  1. Open the photo in Samsung Gallery
  2. Tap the pencil (Edit) icon
  3. Tap the three-dot menu → Object Eraser
  4. Paint over the background you want to remove
  5. Tap "Done" and save

Object Eraser works well for simple backgrounds but struggles with hair, fur, and complex edges. For those cases, use the browser method above.

Method 3: Google Pixel Magic Eraser

Pixel phones (6 and newer) have Magic Eraser in Google Photos. Open the photo in Google Photos → Edit → Tools → Magic Eraser. It removes unwanted objects but doesn't produce a transparent PNG — it fills in the background instead.

If you need a true transparent cutout for stickers or design work, use the browser method. Magic Eraser is better for "remove this photobomber from my vacation photo."

Which Android Phone Has the Best Built-In Background Tool?

Ranking the major Android OEMs on native background removal:

  • Samsung Galaxy: Best — Object Eraser is built into Gallery and works well for simple backgrounds.
  • Google Pixel: Decent — Magic Eraser handles object removal but doesn't output transparent PNGs.
  • OnePlus/Oppo/Realme: Inconsistent — ColorOS and OxygenOS have varying tools depending on the version.
  • Xiaomi/Redmi/Motorola/Nokia: No built-in equivalent. Browser tools are your best bet.

Tips for Better Results on Android

  • Shoot against a simple background. A wall or a piece of paper gives the AI the cleanest edge.
  • Use good lighting. Backlighting (light behind your subject) creates halos. Front-light evenly.
  • Hair and fur need a steady hand. The AI usually gets 90% right; touch up fine details with a free in-browser eraser tool.
  • Process on Wi-Fi. Even though processing is local, the initial AI model download is about 20MB. After that, it works offline.

Common Android Problems (and Fixes)

Android fragmentation means the same tool can behave differently across devices. Here are the most common issues:

  • "Processing takes forever." Older Android phones (2019 or earlier) may lack WebGPU support. The tool falls back to WASM, which is 3–5x slower. Use Chrome and close other apps to free memory.
  • "Downloaded PNG has black background." Some Android gallery apps show transparent pixels as black or checkered. Open the PNG in Google Photos or send it to yourself on Telegram — transparency works correctly in real apps.
  • "Hair looks choppy." Fine hair is the hardest case for any AI model. Shoot against high-contrast background (dark hair on light background, or vice versa). Touch up with a free in-browser eraser.
  • "Chrome says storage is full." The AI model caches locally. Clear Chrome cache if you're short on space, or use Firefox which manages its cache more aggressively.
  • "The tool closes when I switch apps." Android kills background tabs. Keep the browser app open during processing. If you switch away, the job stops and you'll need to restart.

What About Android Camera Apps Built-In Removal?

Some third-party camera apps (like Pixellab, Background Eraser by handyCloset) offer one-tap background removal as you shoot. The advantage: you see the cutout immediately. The disadvantage: quality is usually worse than post-processing with a full AI model, and the free versions are ad-heavy.

For most users, shooting normally then removing the background in a browser tool gives better results than doing it in-camera. The post-processing model has more compute available than a phone's real-time pipeline.

From Android Cutout to Final Use Case

Once you have the transparent PNG, here's where it goes:

  • WhatsApp/Telegram stickers: Crop to a square, add a white border, save. Telegram stickers work best as transparent PNGs.
  • Instagram story overlays: Save the PNG, open Instagram Stories, add sticker from your gallery. The transparent background lets the story image show through.
  • E-commerce product photos: Drop the cutout onto a white or branded canvas in a free editor. Perfect for eBay, Etsy, or Shopify mobile listings.
  • Logos and watermarks: If you have a flat logo on a colored background, remove the background to get a transparent version you can place anywhere.
  • Resume and LinkedIn photos: Replace a cluttered room background with a professional office backdrop. Save as PNG and upload.

Android FAQ

Does this work on Android tablets?

Yes. Chromebooks and Android tablets work the same way — open the browser and process photos on the larger screen.

Is the image quality reduced on Android?

No. The AI model runs at full resolution on your device. The output PNG matches your input dimensions, just with the background removed.

Can I batch process photos on Android?

Browser tools typically handle one image at a time on mobile. For batches of 20+, use the desktop version on a computer. On a phone, processing 5–10 images manually is quick enough.

What if the tool says my browser isn't supported?

Make sure Chrome is updated to the latest version. WebGPU support (which speeds up processing) requires Chrome 113+. Older phones will fall back to WASM, which is slower but still works.

Can I make WhatsApp stickers from this?

Absolutely. Remove the background, save the PNG, then use a sticker maker app (or WhatsApp's built-in sticker feature) to turn your cutout into a sticker. This is one of the most popular use cases.
