Guía Detallada

Step 1: Upload Multiple Images

Click the upload area and select multiple images at once.

Step 2: Reorder Frames

Drag any frame in the preview to reorder it.

Step 3: Adjust Settings

Configure frame delay (50-2000ms), size, and quality.

Step 4: Generate GIF

Click "Generate GIF" to process.

Step 5: Preview & Download

The generated GIF is shown in a player. Click "Download" to save.

Tips for Better GIFs

  • Fewer frames = smaller file
  • Consistent dimensions
  • Shorter delay = snappier
