Guía Detallada

Paso 1: Subir tu Imagen

Sube una imagen JPG, PNG o WebP. La imagen completa aparece en el espacio de trabajo con una superposición de recorte punteada.

The cropper workspace — the dashed overlay shows the area to be kept

Paso 2: Ajustar Área de Recorte

Puedes interactuar con la superposición de recorte de dos maneras:

  • Move: Click and drag anywhere inside the dashed overlay to reposition it over your desired area
  • Resize: Drag the corner handles (small circles at each corner) to adjust the crop dimensions
  • Aspect ratio presets: Use the dropdown to lock to common ratios like 1:1 (Instagram), 16:9 (widescreen), or 9:16 (Stories)
Tip: When a preset ratio is selected, resizing one corner automatically adjusts the other dimension to maintain the ratio.

Paso 3: Recortar y Descargar

Haz clic en "Recortar" para aplicar. La imagen se recorta a tu selección y la superposición desaparece. Haz clic en "Descargar" para guardar como PNG.

Mejores Casos de Uso

  • Social media cropping — Use preset ratios to match platform requirements exactly
  • Photo framing — Remove unwanted edges or improve composition (rule of thirds)
  • Thumbnail creation — Crop to the most visually interesting part of an image
  • Uniform image sets — Apply the same aspect ratio to a series of photos for consistency

Consejos Profesionales

  • Cropping never reduces the quality of the remaining pixels — it simply removes unwanted areas
  • For portraits, place the subject's eyes along the top third line for pleasing composition
  • The 1:1 preset is perfect for Instagram posts; 9:16 for Stories and Reels
  • After cropping, you can use the Resizer tool to adjust to exact pixel dimensions

Preguntas Frecuentes

Usa relaciones: 1:1 Instagram, 9:16 Stories/Reels, 16:9 Twitter/X.
