How to Restore an Old Photo Online

1

Upload Photo

Click the upload area or drag and drop your old or damaged photo (JPG, PNG, or WebP).

2

Choose a Preset

Click a quick fix preset — Auto Restore, Remove Scratches, Reduce Noise, Sharpen Face, or Enhance Contrast.

3

Fine-Tune Manually

Use the sliders to adjust denoise, scratch removal, contrast, sharpen, brightness, and sepia warmth to perfection.

4

Descargar

Enable the before/after slider to compare, then download your restored photo in PNG format.
