详细使用指南

步骤 1：上传图片

Click the dropzone or drag and drop any image file. JPG, PNG, WebP, and BMP are all supported. The image will be loaded onto a canvas for editing.

Step 2: 位置 the Circle

Use the 尺寸 slider to control how big the circle is (10% to 100% of the image). Use the Offset sliders to move the circle horizontally and vertically — useful when the subject is off-center.

Step 3: Choose Background

  • Transparent — Best for avatars and overlays.
  • White — For documents or slides.
  • Custom 颜色 — Match your brand.

步骤 4：下载

Click "下载 PNG" to save. PNG format preserves alpha (transparency).
