Detailed User Guide

Step 1: Upload Your Image

Click the dropzone or drag and drop an image file (JPG, PNG, or WebP). The original image will be displayed and you can preview the flip effect immediately.

Step 2: Choose Flip Direction

Select the flip direction:

  • Horizontal Flip — Mirror the image left to right. Useful for fixing selfie photos or creating mirror effects.
  • Vertical Flip — Mirror the image top to bottom. Creates an upside-down reflection effect.
  • Both — Apply both horizontal and vertical flip at once (180° rotation equivalent).
Tip: Horizontal flip is great for correcting text in mirrored selfies, or creating symmetric mirror effects for social media.

Step 3: Apply & Download

Click "Apply Flip" to process the image, then click "Download" to save the flipped image as a PNG file.

Best Use Cases

  • Selfie correction — Flip a mirrored selfie to show the true scene
  • Mirror effects — Create symmetric designs for logos and social posts
  • Text correction — Fix backwards text in reflected surfaces
  • Art projects — Create kaleidoscope or reflection effects
