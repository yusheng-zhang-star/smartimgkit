Detailed User Guide

Step 1: Upload Your Image

Click the dropzone or drag and drop a JPG, PNG, or WebP image.

Step 2: Choose Blur Mode

Select auto-detect (all faces), Manual (drag rectangle), or Both.

Step 3: Adjust Blur Strength

Use the blur strength slider (5-50px).

Step 4: Preview & Refine

The blurred result is shown immediately. If a face is missed, use manual mode.

Step 5: Download

Click "Download" to save the blurred image.
