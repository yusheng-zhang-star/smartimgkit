详细使用指南

背景移除算法

The studio uses an intelligent corner-sampling algorithm: it analyzes pixel colors at the four corners and edges of your image to determine the dominant background color, then makes all pixels within the tolerance threshold transparent. This approach works exceptionally well for product photography with solid white/gray/colored backdrops. For complex backgrounds (people outdoors, busy scenes), increase the tolerance slider or switch to manual mode.

选择容差

Tolerance controls how aggressively similar pixels are removed. Lower values (10-25) are precise but may leave edge artifacts on slightly uneven lighting. Higher values (35-60) clean up gradients and soft shadows but risk eating into the subject edges. Start at 30 and adjust based on preview results.

边框选项

The border step adds a solid-colored frame around your cutout image. Use it to create consistent product card layouts. Set size to 0 to disable. The radius option creates rounded corners for a modern app-icon look.

阴影效果

The drop shadow adds realistic depth by rendering a blurred dark offset behind your image on a solid background canvas. Increase blur for softer shadows (good for floating products), decrease for sharper contact shadows. Offset X/Y control the shadow direction — positive values push shadow down-right.
