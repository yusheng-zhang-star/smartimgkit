此工作流的作用

Batch Watermark & Protect runs 2-3 operations in sequence on every uploaded image: (1) add a text watermark with configurable opacity and color, (2) optionally overlay an image watermark (your logo), (3) optionally strip EXIF metadata for privacy and smaller file size. The result is one ZIP per run containing all watermarked images.

文字水印与图片水印

文字水印 is best for copyright notices (e.g. "© 2026 Your Name"). 图片水印 uses your own logo or brand image, overlaid at 20% of the width. You can use both at the same time for maximum protection.

EXIF 去除

EXIF metadata includes camera model, GPS location, date/time, and software used. Stripping EXIF makes files smaller and protects your privacy — especially important if you share photos taken with a smartphone (which may include GPS data).
