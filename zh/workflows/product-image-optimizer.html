此工作流的作用

Product Image Optimizer runs 3 operations in sequence on every uploaded product image: (1) compress to JPEG at configurable quality for fast page loading, (2) convert to selected output format (JPEG/WebP/PNG), (3) optionally strip EXIF metadata for privacy and smaller file size. The result is one ZIP per run containing all optimized images, ready to upload to your store.

格式指南

JPEG: 文件最小，最适合网站商品照片。 WebP: 压缩与质量比最佳，所有现代浏览器均支持。 PNG: 无损、支持透明、文件较大。使用 WebP 效果最佳。

压缩技巧

Quality 75-85% gives the best balance of file size and visual quality for product photos. Below 65% may show artifacts on smooth surfaces. Above 90% the file size increases significantly with minimal quality gain.

最佳用例

  • 优化 Shopify/Amazon/eBay 店铺的供应商商品照片
  • 将图片转换为 WebP 以加速页面加载
  • 发布前去除 EXIF 元数据（隐私 + 文件大小）
  • 上传前批量处理商品目录
