What is Image Metadata?

📷

EXIF Data

Camera model, shutter speed, aperture, ISO, focal length, date taken.

📍

GPS Location

Latitude/longitude where the photo was taken. A privacy risk if shared.

🏷️

IPTC / XMP

Copyright, author, keywords, captions —embedded by photo editing software.

📁

Privacy Risk

Metadata can reveal your location, device, and editing history. Remove it before sharing.
