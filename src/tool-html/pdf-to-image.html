📖 How to Convert PDF to Images

  1. Upload your PDF: Click the upload area and select a PDF file. It's loaded entirely in your browser — no server upload.
  2. Choose settings: Pick the output format (PNG for quality, JPG for smaller files), scale factor (2x is good for most uses), and page range.
  3. Convert: Click "Convert PDF". Each page is rendered to a canvas and exported as an image.
  4. Download: Download individual pages or all pages as a ZIP file.

❓ FAQ — PDF to Image

Is there a file size limit?

The tool runs in your browser, so very large PDFs (100+ MB) may cause memory issues. For best results, keep PDFs under 50MB.

Which output format should I choose?

PNG: Best quality, lossless, larger file size. Good for text documents and diagrams.
JPG: Smaller file size, some quality loss. Good for photos.
WebP: Modern format, good balance of quality and size.

Why is conversion slow?

PDF rendering is CPU-intensive, especially at 2x or 3x scale. Complex PDFs with many fonts or graphics take longer. Be patient — it's running on your device, not a slow server.

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser using pdf.js. Your PDF never leaves your device.

Can I convert password-protected PDFs?

Not currently. Please remove the password protection before uploading.
