Lock vs Home screen

Lock screen wallpapers benefit from a dark gradient at the top so the clock and notifications stay readable. Home screen wallpapers show the full image behind app icons. This tool generates both so you can pick the version that looks best.

Share your wallpapers

Beautiful wallpapers are highly shareable on social media. Create wallpapers from your travel photos, AI art, or nature shots and post them — users love collecting custom phone backgrounds.

FAQ

Phone screens are tall and narrow. Cover-cropping ensures your photo fills the screen without black bars. Use the focus option to choose which part stays visible.
For iPad or other tablets, use the Resizer tool with custom dimensions. This workflow targets phone screens specifically.
