Amazon image requirements

Main image must be: pure white background (RGB 255,255,255), product fills 85% of frame, no text/logos/watermarks. Secondary images can show the product in use, dimensions, or details. This workflow enforces the white main image rule automatically while preserving your lifestyle shots.
