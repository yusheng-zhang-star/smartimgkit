Generate every App Store screenshot size in one pass

When you submit an app to the App Store, Apple asks for a full set of screenshots across multiple device sizes — iPhone 6.7", 6.5", and 5.5", plus iPad and more. Cropping each size by hand is tedious, and getting a ratio wrong can mean a rejection or a blurry, badly-cropped gallery. App Store Screenshot Suite takes one well-designed screenshot and generates all the required dimensions automatically, packaged for download.

For indie developers and app marketing teams, this is one of the most error-prone and fiddly steps in the whole submission process. With this tool, one screenshot template covers every required size, which saves the back-and-forth with designers and the manual re-exporting that usually eats up launch day.

Who uses it

  • Indie developers — prepare every required size on your own, fast.
  • App marketing teams — batch out screenshots with a consistent visual.
  • Designers — design one master visual and expand it to all specs.
  • Teams going global — prepare multi-language, multi-size screenshot packs at once.

How to use it

  1. Design one master screenshot at the largest, highest-resolution size.
  2. Upload it and let the tool generate all smaller required sizes.
  3. Review each size for cropping issues.
  4. Download the full set and attach it to your App Store submission.

Build the master at the largest size first

Always design at the biggest resolution you need, then let the tool scale down. Upscaling a small image to a larger size produces blur, while downscaling keeps everything sharp. This single habit avoids the most common screenshot quality problem.

Screenshots are a conversion page, not a manual

The job of a screenshot gallery is to convince someone to download, not to document every feature. The first two or three screenshots decide whether a visitor keeps swiping, so lead with the most compelling visual plus a headline that speaks directly to the user's pain point. Reserve the deeper feature walkthrough for later slides.

Localize the copy, not just the language

When you ship to multiple regions, do not simply translate your screenshot text word for word. Rewrite the headlines so they resonate with each market's users. A master template plus localized copy is the standard setup for any app going global — one visual system, many local messages.

What this tool does not do

This tool sizes and formats a screenshot you design. It does not create the screenshot content itself. The final gallery should still follow Apple's current screenshot guidelines, so review the official requirements before submitting. All processing runs locally in your browser.
