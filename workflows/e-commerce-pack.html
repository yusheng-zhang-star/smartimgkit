What this pipeline does

E-Commerce Pack runs 3 operations in sequence on every uploaded product image: (1) resize to platform specifications (Amazon 1000×1000, Shopify 2048×2048, etc.) with white/transparent background, (2) compress to JPEG at configurable quality for fast page loading, (3) optionally add a text watermark for copyright protection. The result is one ZIP per run containing all processed images, ready to upload to your store.

Platform size guide

Amazon: recommends 1000×1000px (min 500px). Shopify: max 2048×2048px, 800×800px is standard. eBay: max 1600×1600px. Etsy: max 2000×2000px, 5:4 aspect ratio works best. Use "Custom" to set your own size.

Compression tips

Quality 75-85% gives the best balance of file size and visual quality for product photos. Below 65% may show artifacts on smooth surfaces. Above 90% the file size increases significantly with minimal quality gain.

Best use cases

  • Batch preparing product photos for Shopify/Amazon/eBay stores
  • Resizing supplier images to platform specs
  • Adding store watermark to protect copyrighted product photos
  • Compressing high-resolution product shots for faster page loading
