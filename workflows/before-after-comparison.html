Who uses this

Photographers showing retouching work, designers showing redlines, fitness coaches showing client transformations, contractors showing renovation results, and beauty brands showing product effects. The comparison format is proven to boost engagement on Instagram and Behance.

Better than Canva

Canva's comparison templates require drag-and-drop and alignment. This tool auto-aligns both images to the same size (cover-cropping to match), adds the divider and labels, and exports in one click. Free, no account, no watermark.

FAQ
