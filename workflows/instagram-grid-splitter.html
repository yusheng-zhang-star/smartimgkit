Why creators love this

A 3×3 grid creates a larger visual impact than single posts. Brands, artists, and travel accounts use it to tell a story across 9 posts. Most apps charge $2-5 for this feature or require a subscription. Here it's free and runs entirely in your browser.

Pro tips

  • Use a high-res original (2000×2000+) so each tile stays sharp
  • Plan your composition — the center tile is the most viewed
  • Post all 9 tiles within a few days so the grid stays intact
