What this workflow does

Listing Image Suite runs 3 operations in sequence on every uploaded image: (1) resize to ALL selected platform specifications (Amazon 1000×1000, eBay 1600×1600, Etsy 2000×2000, Shopify 2048×2048) with white background, (2) optionally add a text watermark for copyright protection, (3) compress to JPEG at configurable quality for fast page loading. The result is one ZIP per run containing all platform variants for every uploaded image.

Platform size guide

Amazon: recommends 1000×1000px (min 500px). eBay: max 1600×1600px. Etsy: max 2000×2000px, 5:4 aspect ratio works best. Shopify: max 2048×2048px, 800×800px is standard. Select all platforms you sell on — one upload produces all sizes.

Compression tips

Quality 75-85% gives the best balance of file size and visual quality for product photos. Below 65% may show artifacts on smooth surfaces. Above 90% the file size increases significantly with minimal quality gain.

Best use cases

  • Preparing product photos for multiple platforms simultaneously
  • Resizing supplier images to all platform specs at once
  • Adding store watermark to protect copyrighted product photos
  • Compressing high-resolution product shots for faster page loading across all channels
