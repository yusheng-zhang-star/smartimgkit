About This Tool

Transform regular photos into retro 8-bit pixel art style images. This tool works entirely in your browser: it averages your photo into large square blocks, then scales it back up with smoothing turned off, creating that classic video game aesthetic. Great for making Twitch emotes, Minecraft-style avatars, or retro social media graphics.

How to Use

  1. Upload the image you want to turn into pixel art.
  2. Drag the pixel-size slider to set the block size — small blocks keep more detail, large blocks look chunkier and more retro.
  3. Preview the result and download your pixel art graphic.

Frequently Asked Questions

What pixel sizes can I choose?

Use the slider to set the pixel block size anywhere from 2px to 50px. Smaller blocks keep more of the original photo, while larger blocks give a chunkier, more authentic retro look.

Can I use the pixel art for commercial projects?

Yes, you own all images you create with this tool. Use them for Twitch emotes, website graphics, or merchandise without any licensing restrictions.

Does my image get uploaded?

No, the entire conversion runs locally. Your photos never leave your device, so you can even process private photos without privacy concerns.
