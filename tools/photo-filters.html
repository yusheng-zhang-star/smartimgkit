Detailed User Guide

Step 1: Upload Your Image

Click the drop zone or drag in a JPG, PNG or WebP. The image loads onto the canvas and the currently selected filter is applied so you can preview immediately.

Step 2: Choose a Filter

Each preset is a named look — retro, film, black and white, warm, cool and more. Selecting a filter sets it as the canvas filter and redraws the image, so switching between looks is instant.

Step 3: Download

Click Download to export the filtered photo as a JPG. Your original file is left untouched.

Which Filter Should You Pick?

Warm presets flatter skin tones and golden-hour shots; cool presets suit moody or winter scenes; black-and-white and film presets give a timeless, editorial feel. Try a few and keep the one that fits the mood.

How the Presets Are Applied

Each preset maps to a CSS canvas filter that is set before the image is drawn, so switching presets simply redraws the image under the new filter. Because the operation runs on the canvas in your browser, previews are instant and there is no round-trip to a server.

Troubleshooting and Limitations

Presets apply at a fixed strength with no per-filter intensity control in this tool — for that, use the Image Adjustment tool to fine-tune brightness, contrast and saturation before or after. Output is JPG, which flattens transparency and applies light compression, so for a transparent PNG result use a different export path.

When Filters Shine

Influencers and small brands use a consistent filter to keep a feed visually cohesive without a designer. Bloggers apply a quick filter to make phone photos look polished before publishing. Anyone sharing photos socially gets a finished look in one tap instead of juggling several editing apps.
