Detailed User Guide

Step 1: Add Your Photos

Click the drop zone or drag in several images at once. JPG and PNG are supported, and you can add more files before starting.

Step 2: Run the Batch

Start the process and the AI model removes the background from each photo in sequence. A progress indicator shows how far through the batch you are.

Step 3: Download the ZIP

When finished, download a single ZIP containing each cut-out result. Your original files are never modified.

How the AI Removal Works

The tool uses an on-device background-removal model that segments the subject from the background and returns a transparent PNG. The model is loaded into your browser on first use and cached afterwards; the actual computation happens locally, not on a remote server.

Troubleshooting and Limitations

The first run needs a stable internet connection to fetch the AI model, after which it is cached by your browser. Batch size is limited by your device's memory — very large batches or very high-resolution photos may slow down or need to be split. Results are transparent PNGs, which preserve the cut-out edges cleanly.

Who Uses Batch Background Removal

Marketplace sellers prepare dozens of product photos for listings, e-commerce teams batch-clean catalogue shots, and designers strip backgrounds from a whole asset folder before compositing. Running it locally keeps client photos private from end to end.
