Detailed User Guide

Step 1: Upload a Square Image

Drag and drop a square image. PNG with transparency works best.

Step 2: Preview All Sizes

The tool generates all standard favicon sizes: 16×16, 32×32, 48×48, 64×64, 96×96, 128×128, 256×256, and 512×512.

Step 3: Download ZIP

Click "Download ZIP" to get a complete package.

Step 4: Add to Your Site

Unzip the package into your site's root directory. Copy the HTML snippet into your <head> tag.

Why All These Sizes?

  • 16×16, 32×32 — Browser tab and bookmark icons
  • 180×180 — iOS home screen
  • 192×192, 512×512 — Android home screen and PWA splash
