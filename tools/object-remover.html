Frequently Asked Questions

Is this AI object remover really free?

Yes, 100% free with unlimited uses. No sign-up, no watermark, no file size limits.

Do my photos get uploaded to your server?

No. The AI inpainting model runs entirely in your browser via WebAssembly, so your photos never leave your device.

Can I remove watermarks from photos?

You can erase small watermarks, logos, or text overlays by brushing over them; results depend on background complexity.

Does it work on mobile phones?

Yes, it works on any modern mobile browser, including Android and iOS, with no app download needed.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC images are all supported, processed locally in your browser.
