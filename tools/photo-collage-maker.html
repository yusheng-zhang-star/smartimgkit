Detailed User Guide

Step 1: Add Your Photos

Click the drop zone and select several images. They load in sequence, and the collage is drawn once every image is ready.

Step 2: Choose Layout, Gap and Background

Pick a grid — 2×2, 3×1, 1×3 or 3×3 — then set the spacing between cells and the background colour. The canvas is an 800×800 square; images are scaled to fill their cell, so a square source looks best.

Step 3: Download the Collage

Click Download to export a single PNG. Your individual photos are never modified or uploaded.

Tips for a Clean Collage

Use square-cropped photos for a uniform look, keep the gap small (or zero) for a seamless mosaic, and match the background to your brand colour for a polished finish.

How the Grid Is Drawn

The collage renders onto a fixed 800×800 canvas. Each selected photo is scaled to fill its cell and drawn in reading order — left to right, then top to bottom. The gap value sets the space between cells and from the outer edge, and the background colour fills the space behind and between the photos.

Troubleshooting and Limitations

Photos are placed in selection order and cannot be rearranged after the fact without re-selecting them. Images beyond the chosen grid's capacity are ignored, so a 2×2 layout uses only the first four photos. Because the output is a fixed 800×800 square, this tool is optimised for social posts and web use rather than large-format printing.

Who Uses Collages

Small shops build a single product-range image for a marketplace banner. Families combine a trip's best moments into one shareable picture. Creators assemble a before/after or progress shot to show a transformation without a carousel.
