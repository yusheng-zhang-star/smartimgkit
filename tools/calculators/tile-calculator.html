How to Calculate Tile Quantity

We first calculate total floor/wall area by multiplying width × length. Then we convert your tile size from inches to square feet, divide total area by tile area, and add 5% waste for cuts, broken tiles and mistakes. This matches the standard rule of thumb every tiler uses.

Most DIYers forget to add waste, and end up 5-10 tiles short halfway through the job. Since tile is sold by the box, you'll have to buy a whole extra box just for a few tiles, which wastes money. We automatically add 5% so you buy the right number of boxes the first time.

Common Mistakes

Don't forget cuts. Every tile at the edge of the room needs to be cut, so you will break or waste about 1 out of 20 tiles. 5% waste is the industry minimum.

Boxes are sold whole. Tiles almost always come in boxes of 10-15 tiles. Our result rounds up, so you know how many full boxes to buy, not partial tiles.

FAQ

How many tiles for a 12x12 kitchen?

For a 12x12 kitchen with standard 12x12 tiles, you need about 149 tiles (5% waste included). That's roughly 10-11 boxes, depending on how many tiles come in a box.

How much extra tile should I buy?

Always buy 5-10% extra for cuts and future repairs. Tiles from different batches may not match, so keep extra tiles for repairs later.
