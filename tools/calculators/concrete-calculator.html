How It Works

Concrete is sold by the cubic yard. We calculate volume in cubic feet (width × length × thickness converted to feet), then divide by 27 to get cubic yards. We automatically add 10% extra for uneven subgrade, spillage and rounded corners — most concrete trucks will not deliver partial yards, so you'll want extra rather than running short.

For a standard 10x10 patio at 4 inches thick, you need about 1.23 cubic yards of concrete. Most ready-mix companies have a 1-yard minimum order, so this will fit right on the minimum.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much concrete do I need for a 10x10 slab?

A: At the standard 4-inch thickness, you need 1.23 cubic yards, or about 68 80lb bags of concrete mix.

Q: Is 4 inches thick enough for a driveway?

A: Yes, 4 inches is the standard minimum for passenger vehicle driveways. For RVs or heavy truck use, go to 6 inches.

Q: How much does a yard of concrete weigh?

A: One cubic yard of concrete weighs roughly 4000 lbs (2 tons) when freshly poured.

Q: Should I order extra concrete?

A: Always add 10% to your calculated amount. It is much cheaper to have leftover than to run out mid-pour.

Q: How many 80lb bags equal a yard of concrete?

A: It takes about 45 80lb bags to make one full cubic yard of concrete.
