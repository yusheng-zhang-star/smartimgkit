How the Paint Calculator Works

We use the exact same formula every professional painter uses: first we calculate total wall perimeter (2 × room width + 2 × room length), multiply by ceiling height to get total wall area. Then we subtract space taken up by doors (20 sq ft each) and windows (15 sq ft each), multiply by the number of coats you need, and add 10% for waste. One gallon of standard interior paint covers 350 square feet, which matches the label on Behr, Sherwin-Williams and Valspar paint cans sold at Home Depot and Lowe's.

Most DIY painters make one of two mistakes: they either buy too much paint and end up with half-empty cans they never use again, or they buy too little and have to make a second trip to the hardware store halfway through the project. This calculator is built to give you the exact number of cans you need to buy on the first trip.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Don't forget the second coat. Most walls need 2 coats of paint, especially if you're covering a dark color with a light one. If you calculate for 1 coat, you will almost always run out halfway through.

Don't over-buy. You don't need 20% waste for a flat interior wall. 10% is more than enough for touch-ups, uneven patches and spillage. Extra paint you buy will go to waste.

Don't calculate for the ceiling. Most people paint walls only. If you need to paint the ceiling too, add the total floor area to your wall area before calculating.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many gallons of paint do I need for a 12x12 bedroom?

For a 12x12 bedroom with 8ft ceilings, 1 interior door, and 2 standard windows, you need approximately 2 gallons of paint for 2 coats. This matches real-world results from thousands of DIY projects.

Yes. There is no signup, no credit card, no trial period. All our tools run locally in your browser, so our server costs are almost zero — we don't need to charge you.

How much paint do I need for a 10x10 room?

A 10x10 room with 8ft ceilings needs about 1 gallon of paint for 2 coats. We recommend buying 1.5 gallons to leave extra for touch-ups later.

Does this calculator work for exterior paint?

Exterior paint coverage is usually 250 sq ft per gallon instead of 350. You can adjust the advanced waste setting to account for rough siding, or use our dedicated fence/deck stain calculator for outdoor projects.
