Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much gravel do I need for a 10x20 driveway?

A: At 4 inches deep, you need about 2.5 cubic yards, or 3.7 tons of gravel.

Q: How deep should a gravel driveway be?

A: 4-6 inches compacted in two lifts works best for passenger car use.

Q: How much does a yard of gravel weigh?

A: One cubic yard weighs about 1.4 tons (2800 lbs).
