Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much flooring do I need for a 12x12 room?

A: You need 158 square feet total, equal to about 7 boxes (most boxes cover ~22 sq ft), including 10% waste.

Q: How much extra flooring should I buy?

A: Buy 10% extra for cuts. Matching dye lots for future repairs is almost impossible.

Q: How much does one box cover?

A: Most laminate/vinyl boxes cover 20-25 sq ft. Check the label on your specific product.
