Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much stain do I need for a 12x12 deck?

A: You need approximately 2 gallons of deck stain for a 12x12 deck, for one coat, including 10% waste.

Q: How much area does one gallon of deck stain cover?

A: One gallon covers 250-350 sq ft on smooth new wood, and 150-200 sq ft on rough weathered wood.

Q: Should I add extra for railing?

A: Yes, add 20% extra if staining railing and balusters, which use much more stain per square foot.
