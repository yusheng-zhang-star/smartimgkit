Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many rolls do I need for a 12x12 room?

A: For an 8ft high ceiling, you need about 6 standard rolls of wallpaper, including 15% pattern waste.

Q: What size is a standard wallpaper roll?

A: Most US rolls are 27 inches wide by 27 feet long, covering about 60 square feet per roll.

Q: Should I deduct doors and windows?

A: The 15% pattern waste we add already covers small openings. Deduct only for large patio doors or built-in closets.

Q: How much extra wallpaper should I buy?

A: Buy 1 extra roll beyond the calculated amount. It is very common to have small mistakes, and matching dye lots later is hard.

Q: Does pattern repeat increase how much I need?

A: Yes. Large repeating patterns (20+ inches repeat) can waste 20-30% of material. Our default 15% waste works for most small-pattern wallpapers.
