Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many pavers do I need for a 10x10 patio?

A: For standard 4x8 inch pavers, you need about 450 pavers, plus 4 inches of compacted gravel base underneath.

Q: How much base gravel do I need?

A: Use 4 inches of gravel for patios, 6 inches for vehicle driveways.

Q: How much extra pavers should I buy?

A: Buy 10% extra for edge cuts, and keep a few boxes for future repairs.
