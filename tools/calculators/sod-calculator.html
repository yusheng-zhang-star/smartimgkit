Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much sod do I need for a 1000 sq ft yard?

A: You need 1000 square feet of sod, which equals 2 standard pallets. Add 5% extra for cuts around beds and walkways.

Q: How big is one pallet of sod?

A: One standard pallet covers 500 square feet, and weighs about 2500 lbs.

Q: How much extra sod should I order?

A: Order 5% extra to account for cuts and small mistakes during installation.
