Detailed User Guide

Step 1: Add Your Product Photo

Click the drop zone and select a JPG or PNG up to 20MB. The image loads locally and the Generate button becomes available.

Step 2: Generate the Clean Photo

Click Generate Clean Product Photo. The first run downloads the AI model from a CDN, then removes the background on your device and composites the result onto a white canvas. Processing time depends on your hardware and image size.

Step 3: Download

Click Download to save the result as a PNG. The output is a 1600×1600 white-background image with your product centred and scaled to 80%.

Note on How the AI Works

The AI model is loaded from a CDN on first use and cached by your browser, but the actual background-removal computation happens locally on your device. Your photo is never uploaded to any server. A stable internet connection is only needed for that initial model download.

How the Result Is Composited

After the AI removes the background, the cut-out is placed on a 1600×1600 canvas filled with pure white. The product is scaled to 80% of the canvas and centred, leaving a clean margin of white space on every side — the layout most marketplaces expect for a primary listing image.

Troubleshooting and Limitations

The first generation is slow because the model downloads once; later runs reuse it and are faster, but clearing your browser cache forces a re-download. Products with soft or low-contrast edges against a busy background may produce rough cut-outs, since the model performs best on well-lit subjects with clear boundaries. The output is always 1600×1600 PNG — there is no size or aspect-ratio option in this tool.

How It Fits a Listing Workflow

Use this tool to get a clean white-background hero image, then use Square Product Image for strict 1:1 marketplace thumbnails, and Image Compressor to keep the file size under a platform's upload cap. Because everything runs locally, you can prepare unreleased product photos without leaking them to a third-party service.
