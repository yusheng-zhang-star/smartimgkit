Detailed User Guide

Step 1: Upload Your Photo

Click the drop zone or drag in a JPG, PNG or WebP. The image loads into memory for processing.

Step 2: Choose Size and Background

Select the output size (for example 1080×1080) and a background — white, another colour, or transparent. The image is scaled to 90% of the square and centred, so no part is cropped.

Step 3: Download

Click Download to export a PNG. The result is a perfect 1:1 square with your product centred on the background you chose.

Why Square Images Matter

Square images display consistently across marketplace grids and social feeds. A centred product on a clean background also looks more professional than a tight crop.

How the Squaring Is Computed

The tool creates a square canvas of your chosen size, fills it with the selected background, then scales your image to 90% of that square and centres it. Because the scale factor is derived from the image's own width and height, the entire product is always visible — nothing is cropped or stretched out of proportion.

Troubleshooting and Limitations

The output size is fixed to the selected square dimension, so this is a resizing-and-padding tool rather than a background remover; if your photo has a cluttered background, clean it first with the Background Remover. Choosing a transparent background produces a PNG with empty space around the product, which is useful for layering but not for marketplaces that require a solid background.

When to Square Your Photos

Amazon and Etsy listings expect square primary images, and square thumbnails render cleanly in search grids. Social platforms crop to square in many feed layouts, so a pre-squared photo keeps your subject centred instead of letting the platform decide the crop.
