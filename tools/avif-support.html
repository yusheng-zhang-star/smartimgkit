Detailed User Guide

Step 1: Check Your Browser Support

The top of the page shows your current browser and whether it can decode AVIF.

Step 2: Choose Output Format

Select JPG (universal, smaller files), PNG (lossless, larger), or WebP (balanced).

Step 3: Set Quality

Use the quality slider (10-100%) to control output file size.

Step 4: Upload AVIF File

Click the upload area or drag and drop an .avif file. Max 30MB per file.

Step 5: Convert and Download

Click "Convert" to process, then "Download" to save.
