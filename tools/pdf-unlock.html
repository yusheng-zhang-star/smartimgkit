About This Tool

Remove password restrictions from PDFs you have permission to access right in your browser. This tool bypasses printing, copying, and editing locks on PDFs you already own, so you can use the document normally without entering the password every time.

How to Use

  1. Upload the restricted PDF you already have permission to open.
  2. Enter the document open password if required.
  3. Click "Unlock" and download your unrestricted PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you bypass unknown PDF passwords?

No. This tool only works for PDFs you already have the password for — it removes permission locks, not open passwords. We do not support cracking protected documents you do not own.

Where does my PDF go after unlock?

The unlocking process runs locally in your browser. The processed file stays on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.
