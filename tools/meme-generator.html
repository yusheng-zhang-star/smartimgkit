Detailed User Guide

Step 1: Upload Your Image

Click the drop zone or drag in a photo. It loads onto the canvas at full resolution and is ready for captions immediately.

Step 2: Type Your Captions

Enter text for the top and bottom lines. Both use the classic bold Impact-style font with a black outline so they stay readable over any background.

Step 3: Size and Download

Adjust the font size slider to match your image, then download the finished meme. Your original file is never modified.

How the Text Is Drawn

Text is rendered directly on the canvas using a bold Impact-style font, with each line centred horizontally and placed at the top and bottom of the image. The white fill plus black stroke is what gives the meme its signature high-contrast look that survives heavy compression.

Troubleshooting and Limitations

Text is positioned as a single top line and single bottom line, so very long captions may not wrap into multiple lines — keep captions short and punchy. The tool exports the canvas at your image's native resolution, so very large images may take a moment to encode. Output is a PNG or JPG depending on your browser's canvas export.

Who Uses the Meme Generator

Community managers turn brand assets into relatable posts, teachers turn lesson moments into engaging visuals, and everyday users caption reaction photos for friends. The template is simple enough that anyone can produce a polished meme without design tools.
