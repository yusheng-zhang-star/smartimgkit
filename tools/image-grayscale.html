Detailed User Guide

Step 1: Upload Your Image

Click the dropzone or drag and drop an image file (JPG, PNG, or WebP). The original image will be displayed and you can preview the effect immediately.

Step 2: Choose a Color Effect

Select from six built-in effects:

  • Grayscale — Convert to black & white. Great for documentary or minimalist looks.
  • Sepia — Warm brown vintage tone. Classic for portraits and landscapes.
  • Invert — Negative film effect. Reverses all colors.
  • Posterize — Reduces the number of colors for a poster / pop art look.
  • Blur — Softens the image. Useful for backgrounds or dreamy effects.
  • Sharpen — Enhances edges for a crisper look.
Tip: Grayscale is the most searched effect — perfect for creating B&W social media posts that stand out.

Step 3: Apply & Download

Click "Apply Effect" to process the image, then click "Download" to save the result as a PNG file.

Best Use Cases

  • Product photography — Grayscale makes product details pop
  • Art projects — Invert or posterize for creative effects
  • Backgrounds — Blur an image to use as a soft background
