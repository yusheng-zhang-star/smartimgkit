How to Create White Background Product Photos

1

Upload Product Photos

Drag and drop or click to select one or more product images. Supports JPG, PNG, and WebP formats. You can upload multiple product photos at once for batch processing.

2

Escolher Fundo

Select your preferred background: Pure White for Amazon and marketplaces, Transparent for design flexibility, Soft Blue for a professional studio look, or Light Gray for subtle elegance.

3

Process Images

Click "Process All" and the AI will remove backgrounds and composite each product onto your selected background. Processing runs entirely in your browser.

4

Download Results

Download individual product images directly or click "Download All as ZIP" to save all processed product photos in one archive for easy uploading to your store.
