Step 1: Upload an Image

Click the dropzone or drag and drop any JPG, PNG, or WebP image.

Step 2: View Metadata Categories

The metadata is grouped by category: EXIF, GPS, IPTC, XMP.

Step 3: Check for Privacy Risks

Step 4: Remove Metadata (Optional)

What is EXIF?

EXIF is metadata embedded in image files by cameras and phones. It can include GPS coordinates, camera info, and more.
