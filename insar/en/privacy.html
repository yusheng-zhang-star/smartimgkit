Privacy

Short version: all calculations run locally inside your browser. No survey points, CSV files, velocity measurements or location datasets are transmitted to our server, stored on our side, or seen by anyone but you. There is no account, no login and no database in this workbench — by design, not by policy setting.

What happens to your data

  • Computation. Every conversion, classification, validation and report is computed in JavaScript on your device, after the page has loaded. The server delivers static files and plays no part in any calculation.
  • Storage. Your imported points, results and generated reports exist only in the browser tab's memory and are destroyed when you close or refresh the tab. Nothing is written to localStorage, sessionStorage or cookies by the analysis tools; survey data is never persisted.
  • Uploads. There is no upload endpoint in the code. The tools work offline once loaded, and the demo datasets are fetched from the same static host as the pages themselves.

What is stored locally

Only your interface preferences and, where you choose to save them, custom screening thresholds — never points, coordinates or measurement values. Clearing your browser's site data removes these preferences completely.

Third parties

This workbench embeds no third-party analytics, trackers or advertising cookies in its tools. (Should advertising be introduced in the future, this statement will be updated first and the change announced on this page.) The static hosting provider sees ordinary, anonymous access logs — the same records any web server keeps — with no association to your analysis data, because none is ever sent.

Professional confidentiality

Survey coordinates and deformation rates are frequently commercially or contractually sensitive. The local-computation design exists precisely so that you can use the workbench on client data without creating data-processing obligations, cross-border transfer questions or retention liabilities. You stay in control of every copy; exports happen only when you click an export button, to your own device.

Contact

Questions about this statement or the workbench's behaviour: use the contact channel on the main site. If you believe any tool communicates with a server, we want to hear about it — that would be a bug against our own iron rule.

This toolkit provides preliminary geotechnical interpretation only. Results cannot replace licensed site investigation or official geohazard assessment reports. Field verification by qualified professionals is required for all engineering decisions.
