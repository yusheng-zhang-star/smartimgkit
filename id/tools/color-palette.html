Panduan Lengkap

Langkah 1: Unggah Gambar Anda

Unggah gambar JPG, PNG, atau WebP apa pun. Bekerja bagus dengan foto, karya seni, screenshot, atau gambar apa pun dengan warna menarik.

Upload an image and choose how many dominant colors to extract

Langkah 2: Pilih Jumlah Warna

Gunakan slider untuk memilih berapa banyak warna yang diekstrak (3–12). Lebih banyak warna memberikan palet yang lebih kaya; lebih sedikit warna memberikan skema yang lebih fokus dan minimalis.

  • 3-4 colors — Ideal for simple branding and minimalist designs
  • 5-6 colors — Balanced palette for most web and UI projects
  • 8-12 colors — Comprehensive palette for complex designs and illustrations

Langkah 3: Ekstrak & Gunakan

Klik "Ekstrak Warna" untuk menganalisis gambar. Alat ini menampilkan:

  • Color swatches with HEX and RGB values
  • Ready-to-use CSS variables that you can copy directly into your stylesheet

Klik swatch warna apa pun untuk menyalin kode HEX-nya ke clipboard secara instan.

Tip: The first color is usually the most dominant. Use it as your primary brand color, and the rest as accents and neutrals.

Kasus Penggunaan Terbaik

  • Brand color inspiration — Extract colors from a mood board or reference image
  • Website theming — Match your site's palette to a key photograph or product image
  • UI design — Generate accessible color schemes for apps and dashboards
  • Art & illustration — Build harmonious color palettes from nature photos or artwork

Tips Profesional

  • Images with strong, distinct colors produce the best palettes
  • Use the CSS output directly in your project's :root for consistent theming
  • Try extracting from multiple images and combining palettes for unique combinations
  • The algorithm finds visually dominant colors, not just statistically frequent ones
