Panduan Lengkap

Quick Presets

Click Auto Enhance for a one-click improvement. Or try Portrait (skin tone optimize), Landscape (color boost), Vivid (max saturation), Warm (golden tone), or Cool (blue tone).

Manual Adjustments

  • Brightness: Lighten or darken the image (-100 to 100).
  • Contrast: Make darks darker and lights lighter.
  • Saturation: Boost or reduce color intensity.
  • Sharpen: Enhance edge definition (0-100).
  • Exposure: Adjust overall exposure like camera exposure compensation.
  • Highlights: Recover or boost bright areas.

Before/After Comparison

Check "Show Before/After" to enable the slider comparison. Drag the slider handle to compare original and enhanced versions side by side.

Unduh

Click "Download Enhanced" to save your image as PNG. All processing is done in your browser — no data is uploaded to any server.
