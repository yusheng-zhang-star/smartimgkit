How to Create a Social Media Post

1

Choose Your Platform

Click a platform preset button (Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, YouTube, etc.) or enter a custom size. The canvas instantly adjusts to the exact pixel dimensions.

2

Set Your Background

Upload a background image or pick a solid color with the color picker. The background fills the entire canvas at the correct resolution.

3

Add Text & Overlays

Type custom text, adjust font size and color, choose alignment. Enable shadow or outline. Upload a logo or overlay image and position it by dragging.

4

Unduh

Click Download PNG to save your social media graphic at full platform resolution. Share directly to Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, or your website.
