Detailed User Guide

Step 1: Upload Your Image

Click the dropzone or drag and drop a JPG, PNG, or WebP image.

Step 2: Choose Quality

Select "High quality" (default) or "Fast" for quicker processing.

Step 3: Click "Remove Background"

The AI processes your image locally. First run loads the model.

Step 4: Preview the Result

The transparent background is shown with a checkerboard pattern.

Step 5: Download

Click "Download PNG" to save the result.

What AI Model Is Used?

This tool uses @imgly/background-removal (v1.7.0), running entirely in your browser via WebAssembly.
