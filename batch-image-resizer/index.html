Frequently Asked Questions

Do my images get uploaded to a server?

No. All resizing, compression and conversion happens 100% locally in your browser. Your photos never leave your device, which means zero privacy risk for sensitive product photos.

Is there a limit on how many images I can process?

No. Free users can process unlimited images in batches, no signup required. There is no per-batch cap of 10 or 20 files like iLoveIMG or ResizePixel.

Can I resize images for Etsy or Amazon listings?

Yes. Use the built-in Etsy / Amazon 2000x2000 square preset to instantly prepare product photos that meet marketplace size requirements, without paying for a Pro subscription.

Does this tool work on mobile?

Yes. The tool is fully responsive and works on phones and tablets. You can bulk process photos directly from your camera roll without a desktop computer.

Does it remove EXIF metadata automatically?

Yes, all processed images automatically have all EXIF/GPS metadata stripped out during export, so sensitive location and camera data is never included in your final product photos.
